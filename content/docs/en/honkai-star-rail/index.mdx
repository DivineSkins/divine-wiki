<Callout type="warning" title="Updates break mods often">
  Honkai: Star Rail updates about every six weeks, and many updates break some
  character mods. Version 3.2 broke every one of them. After an update, let XXMI
  Launcher update SRMI first, then fix only the mods that still look wrong. The
  update guide below shows how.
</Callout>

## Play with mods

XXMI Launcher installs SRMI, the tool that loads Honkai: Star Rail mods, and starts the game with it. These guides show how to add a mod you downloaded and keep your game working. Mods break HoYoverse's Terms of Service, so read [What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/honkai-star-rail/getting-started/stay-safe) first.
