Sound mods change voice lines, sound effects, or music. They work in a different way from every other mod on this wiki. This page explains the difference, the risks, and the tools. It is an overview, not a step-by-step guide.

<Callout type="warning" title="Sound mods change the game's own files">
  XXMI mods stay in their own folder and leave the game alone. Sound mods
  replace audio files inside the Honkai: Star Rail game folder. That changes the
  game itself, which is a different kind of risk from XXMI mods, and nobody has
  measured it. Read [What's allowed and how to stay
  safe](/docs/honkai-star-rail/getting-started/stay-safe) first.
</Callout>

## How sound mods differ

<img
  src="/wiki-images/honkai-star-rail-more-mods-sound-mods-1.webp"
  alt="Two columns: XXMI mods live in the SRMI Mods folder and never change game files, while sound mods overwrite the game's .pck audio files under StarRail_Data and vanish after a game update or file check"
/>

- Honkai: Star Rail keeps its sounds in Wwise audio packages: `.pck` files that hold `.wem` sounds, inside the game's `StarRail_Data` folder.
- A Star Rail sound mod replaces some of those `.pck` files. Sound tools rewrite them on disk. They do not use XXMI Launcher or SRMI.
- So `F6`, `F10`, and the `DISABLED_` folder name do nothing to sound mods.

Some sound mods come as loose `.pck` files that you copy over the game's own. This wiki does not cover that way.

## What updates and file checks do

- **A game update** replaces the changed audio files with new originals. Your sound mods are gone, and you must apply them again.
- **A file check**, when you repair the game in HoYoPlay or Epic Games, also puts the original files back. Repairing the game is a common crash fix, so expect to lose your sound mods when you do it.
- **Editing by hand** can damage a `.pck` file, one guide warns. Keep a copy of every file before you or a tool changes it.

## The tools

Written for XXAR 1.1.6. Nobody has tested these tools on Honkai: Star Rail 4.7 for this wiki.

<ToolCard
  name="XXAR (Hoyo Games Audio Replacer)"
  href="https://github.com/Entity378/XXAR"
  badge="Untested"
  icon={<Icon name="GitHub" />}
>
  A sound mod manager and creator. Packs Honkai: Star Rail sound mods as `.srar`
  files. Version 1.1.6, GPL-3.0.
</ToolCard>
<ToolCard
  name="HoyoAudioTools"
  href="https://github.com/failsafe42/HoyoAudioTools"
  badge="Creators"
  icon={<Icon name="GitHub" />}
>
  Python scripts that unpack the sounds in a `.pck` file and pack them again.
  The low-level path for creators. Needs vgmstream and Wwise.
</ToolCard>

Good to know:

- **XXAR** lists full support for Honkai: Star Rail in its README (checked on 2026-11-24). If it does not find the game by itself, it asks for the game's `StarRail_Data` folder.
- **XXAR** is a fork of ZZAR by Pucas01. The original has had no update since March 2026 and does not pack Star Rail mods.
- **Wwise** is Audiokinetic's audio tool. It turns your sound files into `.wem`. It needs a free Audiokinetic account.
