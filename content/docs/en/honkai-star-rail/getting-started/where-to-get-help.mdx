## What to put in your question

Helpers can fix a problem much faster when they have these details.

1. **What you see.** Copy the exact error text, or post a screenshot or a phone photo of the text on screen.
2. **Your versions.** XXMI Launcher shows them in its bottom-left corner, for example `LAUNCHER 2.5.0`, `XXMI 1.3.2`, and `SRMI 2.4.3`. Add your game version, such as 4.7.
3. **The banner.** Say whether `SRMI 2.4.3 (PRESS F12 TO SHOW GUIDE)` showed in the first 30 seconds of the game.
4. **What changed last.** A game update, a new mod, or a file you edited.
5. **Your graphics settings.** Say whether **Character Quality** is on **High**. Some mods do not show on lower settings.
6. **Hidden errors.** If a mod fails with no message, untick **Mute Warnings** in the launcher's **SRMI** tab, press `F10` in game, and share the text that shows.

Creators: also post the full error text from Blender's System Console, your XXMI Tools and Blender versions, and the character you are working on.

Crop your UID out of every picture before you post it.

## Report a real bug

| Problem                                            | Where it goes                                                                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| XXMI Launcher fails before the game's login screen | The XXMI Launcher issue tracker, with your `XXMI Launcher Log.txt`. [Report a launcher bug](/docs/honkai-star-rail/errors/launcher-problems#report-a-launcher-bug) has the steps. |
| A problem in game, or with one mod                 | Divine's Discord or the AGMG Discord, not the launcher tracker.                                                                                                                   |
| A bug in SRMI itself                               | The [SRMI-Package issue page](https://github.com/SpectrumQT/SRMI-Package/issues) on GitHub, or the AGMG Discord.                                                                  |

Star Rail has few written guides of its own, and most date from 2023. Many creator guides are written for other XXMI games. Use them only for steps that work the same in every game, such as Blender techniques. Never copy their names, folders, or libraries into a Star Rail mod.

### Spot an outdated guide

Honkai: Star Rail modding changed a lot in 2025. A guide, video, or document is out of date if it:

- installs "standalone SRMI" or runs `3DMigotoLoader.exe` by hand. That loader cannot run current mods. Use XXMI Launcher instead.
- was written before April 2025. Version 3.2 changed how the game poses characters, and every character mod made before it broke. [Fix an old mod by hand](/docs/honkai-star-rail/ini/fix-an-old-mod) explains the change.
- shows `DrawCS`, `$\SRMI\vertcount`, or `$_blend_` in its `.ini` examples. Those lines come from the first 3.2 format, which changed again in June 2025.
- uses `F1` for the in-game guide. The key is `F12`.

## Skip fake help

- **Lookalike sites.** XXMI Launcher comes only from `github.com/SpectrumQT/XXMI-Launcher`. Sites with "XXMI" in their name, such as `xxmilauncher.com`, are not official and host old copies.
- **Fake fixers.** Star Rail updates break mods often, so fixers are in high demand. A "fix" from a new account, in a password-protected archive, that asks for admin rights or replaces `d3d11.dll`, is a red flag. Fake fixers carrying malware have been reported. [Update fix tools](/docs/honkai-star-rail/tools/update-fixers) lists the ones this wiki trusts.
- **Login requests.** No helper, mod, or tool needs your HoYoverse login.

[What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/honkai-star-rail/getting-started/stay-safe) explains how to check a download.
