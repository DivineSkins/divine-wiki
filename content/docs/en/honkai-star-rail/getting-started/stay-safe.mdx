## The risk, in short

<Callout type="danger" title="Mods can cost you your account">
  Mods break the Terms of Service of Honkai: Star Rail. HoYoverse can suspend or
  permanently ban any account that uses third-party software, without warning or
  explanation. Star Rail's anti-cheat is not known to detect mod loaders today,
  but it may start doing so at any time. Bans of players who only used cosmetic
  mods have been reported. The risk is real and cannot be measured. Use mods
  only on an account you can afford to lose. Never use any tool that bypasses
  anti-cheat or gives a gameplay advantage.
</Callout>

## What HoYoverse's terms say

Honkai: Star Rail runs under the HoYoPlay Terms of Service from COGNOSPHERE, HoYoverse's publishing company. Three parts matter for mods:

| Section   | What it says                                                                        |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2(b)(iv)  | You may not use or share programs that "may cause an unfair competitive advantage". |
| 2(b)(vii) | You may not copy, adapt or "otherwise create derivative works" from the game.       |
| 4(c)      | Any derivative work needs HoYoverse's "express prior written consent".              |

The terms also say HoYoverse can deny access "at any time for any reason without prior notice".

Star Rail also has its own Fan Creations Guide. Version 1.0 (April 2023) says HoYoverse "strictly prohibit[s] users from installing, using, or distributing any unauthorized plug-ins" for the game. It never uses the word "mod", but "plug-in" is the closest official word for one. HoYoverse has never said that cosmetic mods are allowed. The choice to use mods is yours.

## How Star Rail reacts to mods

Star Rail uses HoYoverse's kernel anti-cheat. Here is what has been reported, as of November 2026:

- **May 2023:** one player posted that they got a 10-day ban after using a cosmetic mod that added ears and a tail to Stelle. Nothing else about their setup is known.
- **June 2023:** a wave of 7-day bans for "third-party software". Several banned players said they used no tools at all, so it is not clearly linked to mods.
- **2024 to 2026:** no report of a ban for using SRMI, Star Rail's mod loader, was found.
- **March 2026:** the XXMI Launcher developer said Star Rail's anti-cheat did not react to the running mod loader. That is one person's observation. He also said the anti-cheat updates itself, so this can change without a game update.

None of this proves anything either way. Ban messages only say "third-party software" and never name the program, and support will not say what was detected. The original SRMI page itself warned that using it on official servers carries a high risk of a ban.

Keep XXMI Launcher's injection and run-method settings at their defaults.

## Who sees your mods

Mods only change what your own PC draws. Other players should see the normal game. The mod files stay on your PC, but the anti-cheat may still notice the mod loader.

{/* NEEDS HANDS-ON TEST: two PCs, one modded, to confirm that a friend who views the modded player's profile or borrows their support character sees the original model. */}

Your screenshots, videos and streams do show your mods, and often your UID next to them.

1. Hide or crop your UID before you share a modded picture or clip.
2. Never post modded footage in official channels: the official Discord, HoYoLAB, or official subreddits. A player of another HoYoverse game was banned from that game's official Discord for this.

Some mods hide the UID on screen. Use them for privacy only. They do not protect your account.

## Mods we never cover

These mods give you an advantage, or put your account and PC at extra risk. This wiki will never explain, link or show them.

| Type                         | Examples                                                                            |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Chest and collectible guides | Lines, pointers or markers that lead you to chests and collectibles                 |
| Puzzle and minigame helpers  | Mods that solve a puzzle or a minigame for you                                      |
| Seeing more than you should  | X-ray or see-through walls, fog removal                                             |
| Hidden information           | Map overlays, markers on hidden objects, buff timers, enemy health displays         |
| Anti-cheat workarounds       | Anything that hides the mod loader from the anti-cheat or blocks it                 |
| Other out-of-scope tools     | Private servers, FPS unlockers, account and Warp tools, mobile "mods", NSFW content |

Ask one question before you install or share a mod: does it show me anything the normal game hides? If yes, it crosses the line.

## Download safely

### Know what a real mod holds

<img
  src="/wiki-images/honkai-star-rail-getting-started-stay-safe-1.webp"
  alt="Two example mod folders: a normal one with .ini, .ib, .buf and .dds files, and a bad one with an .exe, DLL files, a password-protected archive and a run-as-admin note"
/>

A Honkai: Star Rail mod is a folder of `.ini` files, `.buf` and `.ib` model buffers, and `.dds` textures. Some also hold `.hlsl` shader files or a preview picture.

1. Open the archive with your archive tool before you extract it.
2. Delete the download if it holds an `.exe`, a `.dll`, or anything that asks for admin rights.
3. Check your browser's download list. Make sure the file you got is the archive you clicked, not a stray `.exe` from a fake download button.

### Watch out for fake fixers

Star Rail updates break mods often, so fix tools are in high demand. Fake fixers carrying malware have been uploaded after game updates. Treat a "fix" as fake if it:

- comes from a new account with a single upload,
- sits in a password-protected archive,
- asks for admin rights,
- replaces `d3d11.dll`, the mod loader file.

Search results are also full of fake "mod menus" and "skin packs" on GitHub and elsewhere. They are tools for unfair play, or malware. Never download them.

### Keep your login to yourself

No mod or mod tool ever needs your HoYoverse account login. If one asks for it, close it and delete it.

## Respect other creators

- Credit everyone whose work you use.
- Ask the author before you edit, merge or reupload their mod.
- Never sell mods or put them behind a paywall. No HoYoverse text allows it.

[Other people's work](https://divineskins.gg/legal/content-rules#copyright) in Divine's Content Rules has the details.
