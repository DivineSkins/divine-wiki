You can make every Honkai: Star Rail mod with XXMI Tools, gui_collect, and a texture editor. The smaller tools on this page each save time on one job. Learn the main tools first, so you can tell when a helper gets something wrong.

<Callout type="info" title="Community tools, untested">
  Every tool here is free and comes from a named community author. HoYoverse and
  the SRMI team do not make or support them. Nobody has tested them on Honkai:
  Star Rail 4.7 for this wiki. Several of them edit your `.ini` files, so back
  up a mod before you run a tool on it.
</Callout>

## Blender helpers

These add-ons work next to XXMI Tools. Two more, Robust Weight Transfer and ArmatureXXMI, are on the [XXMI Tools for Blender](/docs/honkai-star-rail/tools/blender-add-on) page.

  badge="Untested"
  icon={<Icon name="GitHub" />}
>
  Imports a dump in fewer clicks, cleans it up, and sets up its materials. By
  Gustav0 and LeoTorreZ. Version 3.0.7.
</ToolCard>
<ToolCard

  href="https://github.com/Seris0/AnimeGameArmatures"
  badge="Untested"

  Armatures for Honkai: Star Rail characters, so you can pose a model and test
  your weights. By Gustav0.
</ToolCard>
<ToolCard
  name="Vertex Color Master"
  href="https://github.com/andyp123/blender_vertex_color_master"
  badge="Untested"
  icon={<Icon name="GitHub" />}
>
  Paints or fills one vertex color channel at a time, for example only the
  outline width. By andyp123.
</ToolCard>
<ToolCard
  name="Additional Batch Rename"
  href="https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/additional-batch-rename/"
  badge="Blender 4.4+"
  icon={<Icon name="Blender" />}
>
  Renames vertex groups, bones, and modifiers in bulk, with find and replace.
  Handy after a weight transfer. By Endertainer007.
</ToolCard>

- [Weight paint a mesh](/docs/honkai-star-rail/models/weight-painting) shows how to test your weights with an AnimeGameArmatures armature.
- Vertex colors control shading and outlines. Keep the original's values where you are unsure, and write colors as hex, such as `FF8080`. [UV maps, vertex colors, and outlines](/docs/honkai-star-rail/models/uvs-and-vertex-colors) explains each channel.
- LEOAlphaPaint by HummyR also paints vertex colors. It dates from 2023 and has had no release since.

<ToolCard

<ToolCard
  name="SwapLock and Global Persist Swapkey"

>
  SwapLock stops typing from flipping your toggles. Global Persist Swapkey keeps
  toggle choices when you rename a mod folder. By Nocturne.
</ToolCard>
<ToolCard
  name="3Dmigoto Mods Merger"
  href="https://github.com/ShadowLemoon/3Dmigoto-mods-merger"
  badge="Untested"
  icon={<Icon name="GitHub" />}
>
  A Python script that merges single, merged, and animated mods into one pack
  you switch with a key. By Qian2333.
</ToolCard>

Good to know:

- **SwapLock** and **Global Persist Swapkey** are explained on [Change a mod's keys and saved settings](/docs/honkai-star-rail/ini/change-mod-keys).
- **3Dmigoto Mods Merger** renames each original `.ini` to start with `DISABLED`. It switches between whole mods: it does not mix parts of different mods. [Combine mods into one merged mod](/docs/honkai-star-rail/ini/merged-mods) covers merging.

## Port mods to another model

<ToolCard
  name="AG Remap (Anime Game Remap)"
  href="https://github.com/nhok0169/Anime-Game-Remap"
  badge="Untested"
  icon={<Icon name="GitHub" />}
>
  Moves a mod made for one model of a character onto another model of the same
  character, with no Blender. By Albert Gold.
</ToolCard>

- AG Remap supports Honkai: Star Rail. Version 5.0.0 is from October 2026. Check its documentation for the Star Rail characters it covers.
- Each outfit is its own model with its own hashes. Never place a paid outfit over its free version.
- Ask a mod's author before you port, edit, or share their mod.

## Icons and light cone art

- **Light Cone Generator** by bruhaintnowayy builds a light cone art mod from your image or video. Its hash list needs an update after major game updates. [Replace character icons and portraits](/docs/honkai-star-rail/ui/replace-icons) shows where it fits.

## Tools to skip

| Skip this                                                         | Why                                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Any `d3d11.dll` or "loader" that does not come from XXMI Launcher | Unofficial loaders are not supported, can break, and put your account at risk.                                        |
| Fixers that are not on the update fix tools page                  | Untrusted fixers can break mods or carry malware. Use [Update fix tools](/docs/honkai-star-rail/tools/update-fixers). |
| Tools that import other creators' finished mods back into Blender | Editing someone's mod needs their permission. Keep your own `.blend` files instead.                                   |
| Tools that pull files out of the game                             | Not covered here. Use the asset repo or your own frame dump.                                                          |
