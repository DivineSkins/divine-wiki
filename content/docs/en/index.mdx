Divine Skins is a creator community building custom skins and mods for live-service games. The wiki collects the guides, tools, and workflows that the community actually uses.

## Games

- [**League of Legends**](/docs/lol): modeling, animations, VFX and bins, the full toolchain.
- [**Deadlock**](/docs/deadlock): hero model swaps, textures, sounds, UI, and the Source 2 tools.

More games will land here as creator communities form around them. Want to start one? Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/DivineSkins/divine-wiki) or come hang out in [Discord](https://discord.gg/divine).
