## The risk, in short

<Callout type="danger" title="Mods can cost you your account">
  Mods break the Terms of Use of Wuthering Waves. Kuro Games can suspend or
  permanently ban any account that uses third-party programs, without warning or
  explanation. Wuthering Waves runs a kernel anti-cheat, and nobody outside Kuro
  knows how it treats mod loaders. Bans of players who only used cosmetic mods
  have been reported. The risk is real and cannot be measured. Use mods only on
  an account you can afford to lose. Never use any tool that bypasses anti-cheat
  or gives a gameplay advantage.
</Callout>

## What Kuro's terms say

Wuthering Waves runs under the Wuthering Waves Terms of Use from Kuro Games. Four parts matter for mods:

| Section | What it says                                                                                      |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 7(2)(e) | You may not use "unauthorized third-party programs".                                              |
| 7(2)(f) | You may not get around "the technical measures we take in any form".                              |
| 12      | The game may contain detection software, and Kuro may collect data on "any unauthorized program". |
| 13(2)   | "The terminated KURO GAMES account cannot be restored."                                           |

The terms also let Kuro warn, suspend or close an account without notice. Kuro has never said that cosmetic mods are allowed. The choice to use mods is yours.

## What players have reported

Wuthering Waves uses a kernel-level anti-cheat called Anti-Cheat Expert (ACE). Kuro has never said what it looks for or how it treats mod loaders.

- **January 2025:** one player said they got a 10-year ban a few hours after first using XXMI Launcher with one skin mod. It came during a wider ban wave. Kuro said the wave was not linked to that month's co-op event and named no cause. The claim is not proven.
- **Ban length:** players report that Kuro's bans for "illegal plugins" run for 10 years. In one case the likely trigger was other low-level software, not a mod, and support said it could not help.

None of this proves that mods get accounts banned. None of it rules it out either.

### What XXMI Launcher changes in your game

To keep mods visible, XXMI Launcher writes some graphics and level-of-detail settings into the game's own settings files. Kuro restricts which of these settings players may change, and its terms ban getting around its technical measures. Nobody outside Kuro knows how it views this.

Keep XXMI Launcher's injection and run settings at their defaults.

## Who sees your mods

Mods only change what your own PC draws. Players in your co-op world should see the normal game. The mod files stay on your PC, but the anti-cheat may still notice the mod loader.

{/* NEEDS HANDS-ON TEST: two PCs in one Wuthering Waves co-op world, one modded, to confirm the other player sees the original model. */}

Your screenshots, videos and streams do show your mods, and often your UID next to them.

1. Hide or crop your UID before you share a modded picture or clip.
2. Never post modded footage in official channels, such as the official Discord. Official channels remove it. A player of another game, Zenless Zone Zero, was banned from that game's official Discord for this.

Some mods hide the UID on screen. Use them for privacy only. They do not protect your account.

## Mods we never cover

These mods give you an advantage, or put your account and PC at extra risk. This wiki will never explain, link or show them.

| Type                        | Examples                                                                                   |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Treasure and puzzle markers | Lines over chests, puzzle and challenge lines, collectible pointers                        |
| Seeing more than you should | X-ray or see-through walls, fog removal                                                    |
| Hidden information          | Map overlays, cooldown timers, enemy health or weakness displays                           |
| Anti-cheat workarounds      | Anything that hides the mod loader from the anti-cheat, blocks it, or defeats a game check |
| Other out-of-scope tools    | Private servers, FPS unlockers, account and automation tools, mobile "mods", NSFW content  |

A second kind of mod exists for this game: `.pak` mods. They need a tool that turns off one of the game's own file checks, a signature bypass, so they break section 7(2)(f) of the terms. This wiki does not cover them. It also does not cover unofficial DirectX 12 loaders.

Ask one question before you install or share a mod: does it show me anything the normal game hides? If yes, it crosses the line.

## Download safely

### Know what a real mod holds

<img
  src="/wiki-images/wuthering-waves-getting-started-stay-safe-1.webp"
  alt="Two example mod folders: a normal one with mod.ini, a Meshes folder and a Textures folder, and a bad one with an .exe, DLL files, a password-protected archive and a run-as-admin note"
/>

A Wuthering Waves mod is a folder with a `mod.ini` file, `.buf` model buffers (usually in a `Meshes` folder) and `.dds` textures (usually in a `Textures` folder). Some also hold `.hlsl` shader files or a preview picture.

1. Open the archive with your archive tool before you extract it.
2. Delete the download if it holds an `.exe`, a `.dll`, or anything that asks for admin rights.
3. Check your browser's download list. Make sure the file you got is the archive you clicked, not a stray `.exe` from a fake download button.

Get the game itself only from Kuro's own launcher, Steam or the Epic Games Store. Never use a repack or a "pre-modded" client.

### Watch out for fake fixers

Fake fixers carrying malware have been uploaded after game updates. Treat a "fix" as fake if it:

- comes from a new account with a single upload,
- sits in a password-protected archive,
- asks for admin rights,
- replaces `d3d11.dll`, the mod loader file.

Search results are also full of fake "mod menus", "skin packs" and currency "glitches" for Wuthering Waves on GitHub and elsewhere. They are tools for unfair play, or malware. Never download them.

### Keep your login to yourself

No mod or mod tool ever needs your Kuro Games account login. If one asks for it, close it and delete it.

## Respect other creators

- Credit everyone whose work you use.
- Ask the author before you edit, merge or reupload their mod.
- Never sell mods or put them behind a paywall. Kuro's terms allow only non-commercial use, and its fan works guideline does not cover software at all.

[Other people's work](https://divineskins.gg/legal/content-rules#copyright) in Divine's Content Rules has the details.
