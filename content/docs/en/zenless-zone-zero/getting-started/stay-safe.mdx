## The risk, in short

<Callout type="danger" title="Mods can cost you your account">
  Mods break the Terms of Service of Zenless Zone Zero. HoYoverse can suspend or
  permanently ban any account that uses third-party software, without warning or
  explanation. Another HoYoverse game already detects mod loaders and kicks
  players. Zenless Zone Zero may start doing so at any time. Bans of players who

  Never use any tool that bypasses anti-cheat or gives a gameplay advantage.

## What HoYoverse's terms say

Zenless Zone Zero runs under the HoYoPlay Terms of Service from COGNOSPHERE, HoYoverse's publishing company. Three parts matter for mods:

| Section   | What it says                                                                        |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2(b)(iv)  | You may not use or share programs that "may cause an unfair competitive advantage". |
| 2(b)(vii) | You may not copy, adapt or "otherwise create derivative works" from the game.       |
| 4(c)      | Any derivative work needs HoYoverse's "express prior written consent".              |

The terms also say HoYoverse can deny access "at any time for any reason without prior notice". HoYoverse has never said that cosmetic mods are allowed. The choice to use mods is yours.

## What is known about Zenless Zone Zero

Zenless Zone Zero uses HoYoverse's kernel anti-cheat. Its Steam store page says so too: "Uses Kernel Level Anti-Cheat".

- We found no report of a game ban for a player who only used cosmetic mods. That is not proof that none happened.
- In March 2026, the XXMI Launcher developer said the anti-cheat in this game did not react to the mod loader. He also said the anti-cheat updates itself, so this can change at any time, even without a game update.
- Bans for "third-party plugins" do happen in Zenless Zone Zero. The reports we found did not involve mods.

Keep the mod loader out of the game folder. The ZZMI page warns that loader files in the game folder can trigger the anti-cheat. XXMI Launcher keeps everything in its own folder, so this only matters if a guide tells you to copy files into the game.

There is no supported way around the anti-cheat. Tools and guides that hide the mod loader from it put your account at risk and are not covered here. Keep XXMI Launcher's injection and run-method settings at their defaults. Community "anti-detection" tweaks are workarounds too.

## Who sees your mods

Mods only change what your own PC draws. Other players should see the normal game. The mod files stay on your PC, but the anti-cheat may still notice the mod loader.

{/* NEEDS HANDS-ON TEST: two PCs, one modded, to confirm the other player sees the original model wherever Zenless Zone Zero shows another player's character. */}

Your screenshots, videos and streams do show your mods, and often your UID next to them.

1. Hide or crop your UID before you share a modded picture or clip.
2. Never post modded footage in official channels: the official Discord, HoYoLAB, or official subreddits. A player was banned from the official Zenless Zone Zero Discord for a modded screenshot that showed their UID.

Some mods hide the UID on screen. Use them for privacy only. They do not protect your account.

## Mods we never cover

These mods give you an advantage, or put your account and PC at extra risk. This wiki will never explain, link or show them.

| Type                           | Examples                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Collectible and puzzle markers | Lines or glowing pillars over chests and collectibles, puzzle helpers                       |
| Seeing more than you should    | X-ray or see-through walls, fog removal                                                     |
| Hidden information             | In-game map overlays, ESP markers, buff timers, counters, enemy health or weakness displays |
| Anti-cheat workarounds         | Anything that hides the mod loader from the anti-cheat or blocks it                         |
| Other out-of-scope tools       | Private servers, FPS unlockers, account tools, mobile "mods", NSFW content                  |

Ask one question before you install or share a mod: does it show me anything the normal game hides? If yes, it crosses the line.

## Download safely

### Know what a real mod holds

<img
  src="/wiki-images/zenless-zone-zero-getting-started-stay-safe-1.webp"
  alt="Two example mod folders: a normal one with .ini, .ib, .buf and .dds files, and a bad one with an .exe, DLL files, a password-protected archive and a run-as-admin note"
/>

A Zenless Zone Zero mod is a folder of `.ini` files, `.buf` and `.ib` model buffers, and `.dds` textures. Some also hold `.hlsl` shader files or a preview picture.

1. Open the archive with your archive tool before you extract it.
2. Delete the download if it holds an `.exe`, a `.dll`, or anything that asks for admin rights.
3. Check your browser's download list. Make sure the file you got is the archive you clicked, not a stray `.exe` from a fake download button.

### Watch out for fake fixers

Fake fixers carrying malware have been uploaded after game updates. Treat a "fix" as fake if it:

- comes from a new account with a single upload,
- sits in a password-protected archive,
- asks for admin rights,
- replaces `d3d11.dll`, the mod loader file.

Search results are also full of fake "mod menus" and "skin packs" on GitHub and elsewhere. They are tools for unfair play, or malware. Never download them.

### Keep your login to yourself

No mod or mod tool ever needs your HoYoverse account login. If one asks for it, close it and delete it.
