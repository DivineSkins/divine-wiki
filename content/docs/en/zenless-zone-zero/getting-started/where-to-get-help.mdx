## What to put in your question

Helpers can fix a problem much faster when they have these details.

1. **What you see.** Copy the exact error text, or post a screenshot or a phone photo of the text on screen.
2. **Your versions.** XXMI Launcher shows them in its bottom-left corner, for example `LAUNCHER 2.5.0`, `XXMI 1.3.2`, and `ZZMI 1.5.2`. Add your game version, such as 3.3.
3. **The banner.** Say whether `ZZMI 1.5.2 (PRESS F12 TO SHOW GUIDE)` showed in the first 30 seconds of the game.
4. **Your store and settings.** Say whether you play on HoYoPlay, Steam or Epic, and whether the game runs in DirectX 11.
5. **What changed last.** A game update, a new mod, or a file you edited.
6. **Hidden errors.** If a mod fails with no message, untick **Mute Warnings** in the launcher's **ZZMI** tab, press `F10` in game, and share the text that shows.

Creators: also post the full error text from Blender's System Console, your XXMI Tools and Blender versions, and the agent you are working on.

Crop your UID out of every picture before you post it.

## Report a real bug

| Problem                                            | Where it goes                                                                                                                                                                      |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| XXMI Launcher fails before the game's login screen | The XXMI Launcher issue tracker, with your `XXMI Launcher Log.txt`. [Report a launcher bug](/docs/zenless-zone-zero/errors/launcher-problems#report-a-launcher-bug) has the steps. |
| A problem in game, or with one mod                 | Divine's Discord or the AGMG Discord, not the launcher tracker.                                                                                                                    |
| A bug in ZZMI itself                               | The [ZZMI-Package issue page](https://github.com/leotorrez/ZZMI-Package/issues) on GitHub, or the AGMG Discord.                                                                    |

### Spot an outdated guide

Zenless Zone Zero modding changed a lot since 2024. A guide, video, or document is out of date if it:

- installs "standalone ZZMI" or 3DMigoto by hand. ZZMI moved into XXMI Launcher in September 2024. Use the launcher instead.
- tells you to download SlotFix, or to put a copy of it in your mod. ZZMI already ships it.
- replaces textures by their own hash instead of with SlotFix. Mods moved to SlotFix around version 2.0, and normal maps cannot be replaced by hash since version 1.3. [Fix an old mod by hand](/docs/zenless-zone-zero/ini/fix-an-old-mod) explains the change.
- tells you to switch **Outline Optimization** off in XXMI Tools. That was right for versions before mid-2025. With XXMI Tools 1.8.2, leave it on.
- was written before version 3.0 (June 2026) for install steps. It misses the Steam version and the game's DirectX 12 option, which mods do not work with.
- uses `F1` for the in-game guide. The key is `F12`.

## Skip fake help

- **Lookalike sites.** XXMI Launcher comes only from `github.com/SpectrumQT/XXMI-Launcher`. Sites with "XXMI" in their name, such as `xxmilauncher.com`, are not official and host old copies.
- **Fake fixers and "mod menus".** A download from a new account, in a password-protected archive, that asks for admin rights, asks you to add it to your antivirus exceptions, or replaces `d3d11.dll`, is a red flag. Fake "mod menus" for Zenless Zone Zero on GitHub ask for admin rights and an antivirus exception.
- **Login requests.** No helper, mod, or tool needs your HoYoverse login.

[What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/zenless-zone-zero/getting-started/stay-safe) explains how to check a download.
