You can make every Zenless Zone Zero mod with XXMI Tools, gui_collect, and a texture editor. The smaller tools on this page each save time on one job. Learn the main tools first, so you can tell when a helper gets something wrong.

<Callout type="info" title="Community tools, untested">
  Every tool here is free and comes from a named community author. HoYoverse and
  the ZZMI team do not make or support them. Nobody has tested them on Zenless
  Zone Zero 3.3 for this wiki. Several of them edit your `.ini` files, so back
  up a mod before you run a tool on it.
</Callout>

## Blender helpers

These add-ons work next to XXMI Tools. Two more, Robust Weight Transfer and XXMI Buffer Export, are on the [XXMI Tools for Blender](/docs/zenless-zone-zero/tools/blender-add-on) page.

  badge="Untested"
  icon={<Icon name="GitHub" />}
>
  Imports a dump in fewer clicks, cleans it up, and sets up its materials. By
  Gustav0 and LeoTorreZ. Version 3.0.7.
</ToolCard>
<ToolCard

</ToolCard>

  href="https://github.com/Seris0/AnimeGameArmatures"

  Armatures for Zenless Zone Zero agents and other games, so you can pose a
  model and test your weights. By Gustav0.
</ToolCard>
<ToolCard
  name="Vertex Color Master"
  href="https://github.com/andyp123/blender_vertex_color_master"
  badge="Untested"
  icon={<Icon name="GitHub" />}
>
  Paints or fills one vertex color channel at a time, for example only the
  outline thickness. By andyp123.
</ToolCard>
<ToolCard
  name="Additional Batch Rename"
  href="https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/additional-batch-rename/"
  badge="Untested"
  icon={<Icon name="Blender" />}
>
  Renames vertex groups, bones, and modifiers in bulk, with find and replace.
  Handy after a weight transfer. By Endertainer007.
</ToolCard>

- Satan1c's **XXMI Toolbox** is its own add-on. It is not the **XXMI Toolbox** panel inside XXMI Tools, which has the same name. It needs Blender 4.2 or newer, and a separate build exists for Blender 3.6.
- [Weight paint a mesh](/docs/zenless-zone-zero/models/weight-painting) shows how to test your weights with an AnimeGameArmatures armature. Each component has its own group numbers, so one armature may not fit every part.
- Vertex colors control shading and outlines. Keep the original's values where you are unsure, and write colors as hex, such as `FF8080`. [UV maps, vertex colors, and outlines](/docs/zenless-zone-zero/models/uvs-and-vertex-colors) explains each channel.
- LEOAlphaPaint by HummyR also paints vertex colors. It dates from 2023 and has had no release since.

## .ini and toggle helpers

<ToolCard
  name="3dmigoto-ini-extensions"

<ToolCard
  name="3Dmigoto Mods Merger"
  href="https://github.com/ShadowLemoon/3Dmigoto-mods-merger"
  badge="Untested"
  icon={<Icon name="GitHub" />}
>
  A Python script that merges single, merged, and animated mods into one pack
  you switch with a key. By Qian2333.
</ToolCard>
<ToolCard
  name="RZMenu"
  href="https://github.com/rayvy/RZMenu"
  badge="Beta"
  icon={<Icon name="GitHub" />}
>
  A Blender add-on that builds in-game menus and toggles into your exported mod.
  Needs XXMI Tools 1.6.9 or newer. By rayvy.
</ToolCard>

Good to know:

- **3Dmigoto Mods Merger** renames each original `.ini` to start with `DISABLED`. It switches between whole mods: it does not mix parts of different mods. [Combine mods into one merged mod](/docs/zenless-zone-zero/ini/merged-mods) covers merging.
- **RZMenu** is a beta. It is reported as tested on Zenless Zone Zero, and its author warns about crashes. Players need nothing extra: the export puts the menu into your mod.

## LOD tool

<ToolCard
  name="LOD Configuration Generator"
  href="https://gamebanana.com/tools/19967"
  badge="Untested"
  icon={<Icon name="GameBanana" />}
>
  Writes the `.ini` overrides for the simpler models agents switch to at a
  distance, so your mod does not vanish far from the camera. By tifaforever.
</ToolCard>

- LOD models have their own hashes. [Test and reload your mod](/docs/zenless-zone-zero/basics/test-your-mod#lod) shows how to spot a mod that is missing them.
- The LOD tool's guide sets **Character Quality** to low while you capture the LOD models. Set it back to **High** when you finish, or mods stop showing.
- Ask a mod's author before you port, edit, or share their mod.

{/* NEEDS HANDS-ON TEST: LOD Configuration Generator on Zenless Zone Zero 3.3 with ZZMI 1.5.2, and which other tools its guide needs. */}

Icons and portraits need no extra tool. [Replace character icons and portraits](/docs/zenless-zone-zero/ui/replace-icons) shows how to make an icon mod by hand.

## Tools to skip

| Skip this                                                         | Why                                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Any `d3d11.dll` or "loader" that does not come from XXMI Launcher | Unofficial loaders are not supported, can break, and put your account at risk.      |
| GitHub "mod menus" and "bypass" tools for Zenless Zone Zero       | Tools for unfair play, or malware. Some ask for admin rights.                       |
| Tools that import other creators' finished mods back into Blender | Editing someone's mod needs their permission. Keep your own `.blend` files instead. |
| Tools that pull files out of the game                             | Not covered here. Use the asset repo or your own frame dump.                        |
