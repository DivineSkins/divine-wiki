Your mod works in your game. These guides help you share it so it works for everyone, credits the people you built on, and keeps working after the next game update. Mods are fan work: never sell one.

## Start here

<PremiumCard
  title="Get your mod ready to share"
  href="/docs/zenless-zone-zero/publishing/publishing-checklist"
  icon={<Icon name="ListChecks" />}
  level="beginner"
  featured
>
  Clean the folder, leave SlotFix and RabbitFX out, write a readme, take preview
  shots, credit everyone, and test the archive like a player.
</PremiumCard>
