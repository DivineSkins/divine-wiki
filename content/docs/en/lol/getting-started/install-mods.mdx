<LevelPill level="beginner" />

Celestial installs League mods, keeps them updated after patches, and loads them when you play. This page shows how to install one, turn it on, and play with it.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Install Celestial

Download [Celestial](https://divineskins.gg/install-mods) and run the installer. Celestial finds your League folder on its own.

</Step>
<Step>

### Install a mod

Pick one way:

- **From Divine:** open a mod's page on [divineskins.gg](https://divineskins.gg) and click **Install with Celestial**.
- **In Celestial:** open the store tab, find a mod and install it there.
- **From a file:** open the **Library** and drag a `.modpkg` or `.fantome` file onto it. Or click **Import**, then **Import Files**.

New mods show up in your **Library**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Turn it on and play

1. In the **Library**, click a mod's card to turn it on. The counter next to **Start Patcher** shows how many mods are on.
2. Click **Start Patcher**.
3. Start League and play. Your mods load in game.

Two mods for the same champion? The one higher in your Library wins. Drag mods to change their order.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Mod layers

Some mods come with variants, like chromas. A layers badge on the card shows how many are on. Click it to switch variants on and off. Only the base layer starts on.

## After a patch

Celestial updates Divine mods by itself when their creators post a fix. A mod that broke stays broken until then. If Celestial warns that a mod will likely crash your game, turn it off until its creator posts an update.
