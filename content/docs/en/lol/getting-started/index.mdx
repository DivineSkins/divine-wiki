New to League modding? This page shows what a mod is, what you can make, and which guide to read first. You don't need any tools yet.

## What a League mod is

A League mod is one `.modpkg` file. It holds new copies of game files, such as a champion's model, textures or sounds. [Celestial](https://divineskins.gg/install-mods) loads them over the game's own files when you play.

Mods are client-side: they change the game on your PC only. You see your custom skin, and nobody else does. [Mod format](/docs/lol/mod-format) explains the file.

## What you can make

- Custom skins and chromas for any champion.
- Custom VFX and particles.
- Custom animations.
- Sounds and voice lines.
- Map mods, HUD and interface mods.

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordions type="multiple">

<Accordion title="Are custom skins allowed?">

It's a gray area. Riot doesn't sanction custom skins, and nobody outside Riot knows what its anti-cheat checks. The choice to use mods is yours. We don't recommend using mods on League's Korean or Chinese servers.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do other players see my custom skin?">

No. Mods change the game on your PC only. You see your skin, and everyone else sees the champion's normal look.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do mods break after a patch?">

Sometimes. When a creator posts a fix, Celestial updates the mod for you.

</Accordion>

</Accordions>
