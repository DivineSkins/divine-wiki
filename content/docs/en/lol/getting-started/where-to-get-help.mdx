Stuck on a mod? Ask in Divine's Discord, or check the error pages on this wiki.

## Ask on Discord

Still have questions? Hit us up on Discord. We're always around.

<GlowCTA href="https://discord.gg/divineskins">Join Discord</GlowCTA>

When you ask, include:

- The exact error text, or a screenshot of it.
- What changed last: a new mod, a patch, or a file you edited.
- The mod's name, and whether you installed it from Divine or from a file.
