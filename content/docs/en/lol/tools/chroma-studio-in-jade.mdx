Chroma Studio is a recoloring tool inside [Jade](/docs/lol/tools/modding-apps/jade).
Use it to select one part of a model and change its colors without touching the rest.

## Before you start

- Install [Jade](/docs/lol/tools/modding-apps/jade).
- Know which skin you want to recolor.

## Open Chroma Studio

1. Click **Open file** in the sidebar.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/jade.png"
  alt="Jade home screen with Open file highlighted in the sidebar"
/>

2. Load the skin you want to recolor.
3. Click the highlighted button in the toolbar to open Chroma Studio.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/jade2.png"
  alt="Jade editor toolbar with the Chroma Studio button highlighted"
/>

## Learn the layout

- **Left side:** the textures the mesh uses.
- **Right side:** the settings for your changes.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/jade-3.png"
  alt="Chroma Studio with the mesh textures on the left and the settings on the right"
/>

## Select an area of the model

The right side shows the UV islands. A UV island tells the model which area of the texture to use for each part of the model.
This is why League textures, and most model textures, look flat.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/jade-4.png"
  alt="A UV island shown on the right side of Chroma Studio"
/>

1. Click any area of the mesh.
2. Check that the area is highlighted on the right and on the model.
3. Change the settings. Your changes only affect that area.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/jade-5.png"
  alt="A selected area highlighted on the texture and on the model"
/>

## Adjust the colors

Every setting below only changes the area you selected.

### Hue, saturation, and lightness

- **Hue:** changes the color, for example from red to blue.
- **Saturation:** changes how strong the color is.
- **Lightness:** changes how dark or white the area is.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/screenshot-2026-11-16-151518.png"
  alt="Hue, saturation, and lightness settings in Chroma Studio"
/>

### Color balance

Color balance is the most useful setting.
It shifts the color between each primary color and its opposite on the RGB scale.

- **Shadows:** changes the tint of the darkest parts of the area.
- **Midtones:** changes the tint of the middle brightness range.
- **Highlights:** changes the tint of the brightest parts of the area.

Move the sliders until the color looks right to you.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/screenshot-2026-11-16-151816.png"
  alt="Color balance settings in Chroma Studio"
/>

### Temperature and tint

Temperature and tint control the white balance.
Temperature moves the colors between warm and cool. Tint moves them between green and magenta.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/screenshot-2026-11-16-151939.png"
  alt="Temperature and tint settings in Chroma Studio"
/>

### Brightness

Brightness sets how bright the area is.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/screenshot-2026-11-16-152005.png"
  alt="Brightness setting in Chroma Studio"
/>

### Tone

These settings change the brightness, shadows, and contrast of the area. They don't change its color.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/screenshot-2026-11-16-152117.png"
  alt="Tone settings in Chroma Studio"
/>

### Shadows and highlights

These settings bring back detail in only the brightest and darkest parts of the area.
The midtones stay the same.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/screenshot-2026-11-16-152153.png"
  alt="Shadows and highlights settings in Chroma Studio"
/>

### Replace a color

Pick one color in the texture and change it to a different color.
You can also isolate it. The rest of the texture stays the same.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/screenshot-2026-11-16-152220.png"
  alt="Color replace settings in Chroma Studio"
/>

### Match a color

Pick a color. Chroma Studio changes the area to match it as closely as it can.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/screenshot-2026-11-16-152323.png"
  alt="Color match settings in Chroma Studio"
/>

### Gradient map, black and white, and transparency

- **Gradient map:** replaces the original colors with a two-color gradient, based on how bright each part is.
- **Black and white:** turns the area black and white.
- **Transparency:** changes how see-through the area is.
