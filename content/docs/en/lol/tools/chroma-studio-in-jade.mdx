Chroma Studio is a recoloring tool inside [Jade](/docs/lol/tools/modding-apps/jade).
Use it to select one part of a model and change its colors without touching the rest.

## Before you start

- Install [Jade](/docs/lol/tools/modding-apps/jade).
- Extract the skin you want to recolor, so you have its `.skn` file and textures.

## Open Chroma Studio

1. Click **Open file** in the sidebar.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/jade.png"
  alt="Jade home screen with Open file highlighted in the sidebar"
/>

2. Click the highlighted button in the toolbar to open Chroma Studio.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/jade2.png"
  alt="Jade editor toolbar with the Chroma Studio button highlighted"
/>

Chroma Studio opens empty until you load a model.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/chroma-studio-empty.webp"
  alt="Empty Chroma Studio tab waiting for a model"
/>

## Load a model

1. Click **Load SKN…** in the **Assets** panel. You can also drop a `.skn` file on the viewport.
2. Pick the skin's `.skn` file.
3. Check that the textures the model uses appear in the **Assets** panel.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/chroma-studio-loaded.webp"
  alt="Chroma Studio with a model loaded and its textures listed in the Assets panel"
/>

## Learn the layout

- **Assets** (left): the model and the textures it uses.
- **Viewport** (middle): a live preview of the model.
- **Mesh** (bottom): the parts of the model, called submeshes.
- **Recolor** (right): the settings for your changes.
- **Texture** and **UV** (far right): the texture and its UV islands.

## Select an area of the model

A UV island is one flat piece of the texture. It tells the model which area of the texture to use for each part of the model.
This is why League textures, and most model textures, look flat.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/chroma-studio-uv-island.webp"
  alt="A UV island highlighted in the UV panel"
/>

1. Choose **Island** in the **UV** panel.
2. Click the island you want to recolor.
3. Check that the area is highlighted on the texture and on the model.

The top of the **Recolor** panel shows what you're editing. If nothing is selected, your changes hit the whole texture.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/chroma-studio-island-selected.webp"
  alt="A selected island highlighted on the texture, with Editing 1 islands shown in the Recolor panel"
/>

## Adjust the colors

Tick the box next to a setting to turn it on.
Every setting only changes the area you selected.

### Hue / Saturation

- **Hue:** changes the color, for example from red to green.
- **Saturation:** changes how strong the color is.
- **Lightness:** changes how dark or white the area is.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/chroma-studio-hue-saturation.webp"
  alt="Hue / Saturation turned on, recoloring the selected area green"
/>

### Color Balance

Color Balance is the most useful setting.
Each slider shifts the color between a primary color and its opposite: cyan and red, magenta and green, yellow and blue.

- **Shadows:** changes the tint of the darkest parts of the area.
- **Midtones:** changes the tint of the middle brightness range.
- **Highlights:** changes the tint of the brightest parts of the area.

Move the sliders until the color looks right to you.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/chroma-studio-color-balance.webp"
  alt="Color Balance sliders for shadows, midtones, and highlights"
/>

### Temperature / Tint

Temperature and tint control the white balance.
Temperature moves the colors between warm and cool. Tint moves them between green and magenta.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/chroma-studio-temperature-tint.webp"
  alt="Temperature / Tint sliders"
/>

### Brightness / Contrast

- **Brightness:** sets how bright the area is.
- **Contrast:** sets how far apart the dark and light parts are.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/chroma-studio-brightness-contrast.webp"
  alt="Brightness / Contrast sliders"
/>

### Levels

Levels changes the brightness, shadows, and contrast of the area. It doesn't change its color.

- **Black:** sets how dark the darkest parts get.
- **Gamma:** moves the midtones lighter or darker.
- **White:** sets how bright the brightest parts get.

<img src="/wiki-images/chroma-studio-levels.webp" alt="Levels sliders" />

### Highlights / Shadows

Brings back detail in only the brightest and darkest parts of the area.
The midtones stay the same.

### Color Accent

Pick one color in the texture and change it to a different color.
You can also isolate it. The rest of the texture stays the same.

### Color Match

Pick a color. Chroma Studio changes the area to match it as closely as it can.

### Gradient Map, Black and White, and Transparency

- **Gradient Map:** replaces the original colors with a two-color gradient, based on how bright each part is.
- **Black and White:** turns the area black and white.
- **Transparency:** changes how see-through the area is.
