## Open your skin BIN

Open Jade and click **Open file** in the sidebar. Open your skin's `.bin` file, for example `skin0.bin`.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/jade.webp"
  alt="Jade home screen with the Open file button highlighted"
/>

## Find the skin scale

Press `Ctrl + F` and search for `skinScale`. It sits inside `skinMeshProperties`.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/skinscale.webp"
  alt="skinScale set to 1.2 inside skinMeshProperties in Jade"
/>

Can't find it? Your skin uses the default size. Add this line inside `skinMeshProperties`:

```py
skinScale: f32 = 1
```

## Change the size

`1` is the normal size. Lower numbers shrink the skin and higher numbers grow it.

- **Too big?** Try `0.8` or `0.7`.
- **Too small?** Try `1.3` or `1.6`.

There's no perfect value. Pick what looks right to you.

## Save and test

Press `Ctrl + S` to save the BIN. Pack the mod and test it in-game. Keep adjusting the value until you're happy with the size.
