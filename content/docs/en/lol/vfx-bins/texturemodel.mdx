## Why your submesh looks wrong

Does your new submesh show up white, broken, or with the champion's default textures? League doesn't know which texture belongs to it yet. You fix that by adding an entry in the skin's BIN.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/ugly.webp"
  alt="Sett in-game with a custom submesh showing the wrong textures"
/>

## Open your skin BIN

Open Jade and click **Open file** in the sidebar. Open your skin's `.bin` file, for example `skin0.bin`.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/jade.webp"
  alt="Jade home screen with the Open file button highlighted"
/>

## Open the texture insert panel

Click the picture icon in the toolbar at the top.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/jade2.webp"
  alt="skin0.bin open in Jade with the picture icon in the toolbar highlighted"
/>

## Link the texture to your submesh

1. Find your submesh under **Submeshes from SKN**. A submesh without a texture shows **no match**.
2. Click your submesh, then click its texture under **Textures from folder**. You can also type them into **Texture path** and **Submesh name**.
3. Click **Add entry**.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/jade-3.webp"
  alt="Texture insert panel with a submesh marked no match and a list of textures from the folder"
/>

<Callout type="warning" title="Only .dds or .tex">
  The texture has to be a `.dds` or `.tex` file. Any other format won't work.
</Callout>

## Save and test

Press `Ctrl + S` to save the BIN. Pack the mod and test it in-game to check the new texture.
