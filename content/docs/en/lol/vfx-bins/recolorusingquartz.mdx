## Open your BIN in Paint

Open Quartz and select the brush icon at the top of the sidebar to open **Paint**. Click **Open Bin** and open your skin's VFX `.bin` file.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/uno.webp"
  alt="Quartz with the Paint brush icon and the Open Bin button highlighted"
/>

## Find the effects you want

Once the BIN loads, Quartz lists every particle system with its emitters. Type a code into **Filter systems** to show only one ability.

| Type this | To find          |
| --------- | ---------------- |
| `_Q_`     | Q ability VFX    |
| `_W_`     | W ability VFX    |
| `_E_`     | E ability VFX    |
| `_R_`     | R ability VFX    |
| `_BA_`    | Basic attack VFX |
| `_Z_`     | Idle VFX         |

Tick the checkbox next to each system or emitter you want to recolor.

## Pick your colors

Click the color bar at the top to open the color picker, then choose a color. Want a gradient? Drag the **Colors** slider to add more colors.

## Recolor and save

Click **Recolor Selected** in the bottom right, then click **Save Bin**.

## Test your changes

Preview the result in [Ruby](/docs/lol/tools/modding-apps/ruby), or pack the mod and test it in-game. Ruby is faster, since you don't need to launch a match.
