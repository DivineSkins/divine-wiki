Most custom models look **flat** in game. The texture is fine, but there is no
light or shadow on it, so it looks pasted onto the champion.

A shading material fixes that. With [Ruby](/docs/lol/tools/modding-apps/ruby)
it takes a few clicks, and you see the result live before you open the game.

It works on any skin, not only anime characters.

## 1. Install the preset

1. Download **[AnimeShade](/wiki-files/AnimeShade.rubyshader)**.
2. In Ruby, click the **Browse League / TFT skins** icon (the last icon in the
   top bar) and open the **Shaders** tab.
3. Click **Install a pack** and pick the file you downloaded.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/better-shading-install.webp"
  alt="Ruby Shaders tab with the Install a pack button highlighted"
/>

You only do this once. The preset stays in Ruby for every future skin.

## 2. Open your skin

1. Click **Open BIN** (the folder icon) and pick your skin's `skin0.bin` from
   your **extracted** mod folder.
2. Click **BIN editor** in the bottom right corner and go to the **Material**
   tab.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/better-shading-open.webp"
  alt="Ruby with the Open BIN and BIN editor buttons highlighted"
/>

## 3. Create the material

Click **More** at the bottom of the BIN editor, then:

1. **New Material**.
2. Later, once it's created, **Make name unique**. This stops it clashing with
   other mods that use the same preset.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/better-shading-new-material.webp"
  alt="The More menu with New Material and Make name unique highlighted"
/>

In the **New material** window, pick **AnimeShade** as the preset and click
**Create**. If you see a **Fade in brush, camouflage and on death** box, leave
it ticked.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/better-shading-preset.webp"
  alt="The New material window with the AnimeShade preset highlighted"
  width={600}
/>

Ruby links the shader to your skin for you. Now click **More → Make name
unique**.

<Callout type="info" title="Why the fade box matters">
  The box only appears with an older copy of the preset. Without it your
  champion stays fully visible when it walks into a bush (and during camouflage
  or its death fade). The current AnimeShade download already fades, so with it
  there is no box to tick.
</Callout>

## 4. Set your texture

Under **Textures**, paste your model's texture path into **Diffuse_Texture**
and click **Set**. It's the same `.tex` your skin already uses.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/better-shading-texture.webp"
  alt="The Diffuse_Texture slot on the new material"
/>

## 5. Put it on your model

Open the **Mesh** tab at the bottom of the BIN editor. Under **Base material**,
click **Apply**. That puts the material on your whole model.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/better-shading-mesh.webp"
  alt="The Mesh tab with the Apply button under Base material highlighted"
/>

The preview updates right away. Click **Save**.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/better-shading-result.webp"
  alt="Illumi with the shading material applied in Ruby"
/>

<Callout type="info" title="Only want it on some parts?">
  Each row under **Mesh assignment** is one part of the model. Click **Apply**
  on a row to shade only that part. Skip parts that already use a special Riot
  material, like a transformation or a death dissolve: replacing it removes that
  effect.
</Callout>

## 6. Export the shader into your mod

Keep your skin open, go back to the **Shaders** tab and click **Export to mod**
on AnimeShade.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/better-shading-export.webp"
  alt="The export button on the AnimeShade preset"
/>

Ruby writes a `ShaderCache.dx11.wad.client` **folder** next to your champion
WAD, in the **same layer**:

```plaintext
content/base/
    Locke.wad.client/
    ShaderCache.dx11.wad.client/
```

Ruby picks the place from the skin you have open:

- **Your mod has one layer** (or it's a plain extracted folder): the shader goes
  straight next to your champion WAD.
- **Your mod has several layers**: Ruby asks which one. Your skin's own layer is
  highlighted. Pick that one.
- **You imported your own extracted folder into the Celestial Creator Hub**:
  keep editing that folder as usual. Ruby finds the Creator Hub project that
  copies it and puts the shader where Reload carries it over, and Reload keeps
  it there.
- **You imported a mod project** (a folder with `mod.config.json`): Ruby writes
  into that project. Drop the project into the Creator Hub again to update its
  copy, the same as after any other edit.
- **Ruby can't tell where your skin goes** (no skin open, or a skin outside any
  mod): Ruby asks for a folder. Pick the one that **holds** your champion's
  `.wad.client` (for a mod project, `content/base`).

<Callout type="danger" title="Always ship both, in the same layer">
  Your skin now uses the shader. If `ShaderCache.dx11.wad.client` is missing, or
  sits in a layer that's switched off, **the game crashes on load**. Ruby's
  preview won't warn you.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="Have an old ShaderCache.dx11.wad.client file?">
  Older Ruby versions saved a single packed file instead of a folder, and most
  mod tools don't build a packed file sitting in a layer. Click **Export to
  mod** again: Ruby unpacks the old file into the folder and keeps the old file
  as a `.bak` backup. In a mod project the backup goes to the project's top
  folder, outside `content/`, so it never ends up in your mod.
</Callout>

That's it. Pack your mod and enjoy the new look in game.

## Make it yours

Every setting is under **Parameters** in the Material tab and updates live:

| Setting                                  | What it does                                               |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Saturation`                             | Color strength.                                            |
| `Shading_Threshold` / `Shading_Softness` | Where the shadow starts and how soft its edge is.          |
| `Shadow_Color`                           | Tint of the shadow side.                                   |
| `Rim_Intensity`                          | Soft light around the model's edges.                       |
| `Specular_Intensity`                     | Shine on highlights.                                       |
| `Base_Color`                             | Multiplies the whole texture. Lower it to darken the skin. |

## Still solid in a bush?

If you made your material before the fade box existed, your champion stays fully
visible in brush. To fix it:

1. Open the skin and go to the **Material** tab.
2. Select the AnimeShade material. Its pass shows a warning that it is opaque.
3. Click **Make it fade**, then **Save**.

Do this for every material that uses AnimeShade.
