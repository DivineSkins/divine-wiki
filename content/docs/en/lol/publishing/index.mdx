Your mod works in your game. This guide helps you upload it to Divine. What you can post, and how to credit other people's work, is in [Divine's Content Rules](https://divineskins.gg/legal/content-rules).

You upload League mods to Divine from the Creator Panel. Players install them with [Celestial](https://divineskins.gg/install-mods), and Celestial updates them for players when you post a new release.
