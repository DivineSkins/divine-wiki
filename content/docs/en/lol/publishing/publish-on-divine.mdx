Divine is where you share your mod with players. This page walks you through the upload, from a finished mod to a mod in review. If your mod uses anyone else's work, read [Other people's work](https://divineskins.gg/legal/content-rules#copyright) in Divine's Content Rules before you start.

## Before you upload

1. **Repath champion mods.** [Repath your mod](/docs/lol/getting-started/repath-your-mod) shows how.
2. **Export a `.modpkg`.** Divine takes League mods as `.modpkg` only. Got a `.fantome`? [Convert it](/docs/lol/mod-format/fantome).
3. **Put variants in layers.** A League mod is one file. Chromas and alternate versions go in [layers](/docs/lol/mod-format/layers) inside it.
4. **Test it in game** with no other mods on.
5. **Check the League rules.** Divine doesn't accept:
   - skin hacks: anything that gives you a paid skin for free, including mods that load a paid skin's files in place of another skin
   - a paid skin or chroma posted as it is, recolored, or moved onto another champion
   - a part of a paid skin, such as its model, textures, effects, animations, sounds or voice lines, used without changes
   - anything players would recognize straight away as a paid skin

   Changed parts of paid skins in a new design of your own are fine, as long as the result doesn't pass for the paid skin. So are new skins inspired by a paid skin's theme or style. [Paid skins](https://divineskins.gg/legal/content-rules#league-of-legends-paid-skins) in the Content Rules has the details.

6. **Keep the page safe for work.** The title, description, thumbnail and screenshots must be SFW. Only the mod's files can have 18+ content, and then you mark the mod 18+.
7. **Note where AI helped** with any part of the mod or its page. The upload form asks about it.

## Upload it on Divine

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create your account

Skip this step if you already have an account.

1. Open [divineskins.gg](https://divineskins.gg) and click **Log in** at the top right.
2. Click **Create one here**, or use the Google or Discord button.
3. Open the email from Divine and click the link in it. You can't log in until you do.

</Step>
<Step>

### Start a new mod

1. Click your profile picture at the top right, then **Creator panel**. Or open [divineskins.gg/creator-panel](https://divineskins.gg/creator-panel).
2. Click **Create mod**.
3. Under **Game**, pick **League of Legends**.
4. Type a name that says what the mod is, like "Demonic Kled". You can change it later.
5. Click **Create draft**. The upload form opens.

The form has five steps in a list on the left. **Basic info**, **Files**, **Media** and **Content labels** are required. **Description** is optional, but players read it. Divine saves as you go. Click **Save and exit** to come back later from **Drafts**.

<Callout type="info" title="Your first mod">
  New creators work on one draft at a time. After you submit it, you can start
  your next mod once a moderator publishes the first one.
</Callout>

</Step>
<Step>

### Fill in Basic info

1. Pick a **Category**, like **Champion Mod**, **Map Mod**, **HUD/UI** or **Sound Effects**. Pick a **Subcategory** if one fits.
2. For a champion mod, pick the **Champion**. Champion mods need one.
3. Under **Works on**, pick the skin your mod replaces, **Default Skin** or **All Skins**.
4. Add **Features** and **Themes** if they fit. Pick **Chroma** as a theme to add **Colors**.
5. Add a few **Tags**: type each one and press Enter.
6. Pick a **License**. CC BY-NC 4.0 is set for you.

</Step>
<Step>

### Upload your .modpkg

1. Click **Next: Files**.
2. Drop your `.modpkg` on the box, or click the box to pick it. The box shows your size limit.
3. Keep **Main** as the file name, or type a short name.
4. Click **Upload file**.

You can switch steps while it uploads. Keep the tab open until it's done. The file then shows under **Uploaded**, with its **File check** below it. [The file check](#the-file-check) explains what you see there.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add a thumbnail and screenshots

1. Click **Next: Media**.
2. Under **Thumbnail**, add one image of this mod. Use 16:9, at least 960×540 pixels.
3. Under **Gallery**, click **Add images**. Add screenshots from a few angles, and at least one from in game.
4. Optional: paste a YouTube link to a short in-game clip. Clips show animations and effects that screenshots can't.

</Step>
<Step>

### Answer the content questions

1. Click **Next: Content labels**.
2. **Is this mod NSFW (18+)?** Answer **No** for most mods. If the mod's files have nudity, gore or other 18+ content, answer **Yes** and pick every category that fits.
3. **Did AI make or help make any part of this mod or its page?** Answer **No** if you made everything without AI. If AI helped, answer **Yes** and set each part to the level that fits.

The [18+ rules](https://divineskins.gg/legal/content-rules#adult) and the [AI rules](https://divineskins.gg/legal/content-rules#generative-ai) explain each answer.

</Step>
<Step>

### Write a description

1. Click **Next: Description**, then **Write description**.
2. Say what the mod changes and which skin it replaces.
3. Credit yourself, every co-author, and every creator whose work is in your mod.
4. Click **Save**.

**Template** fills in a starting layout. Under **Links**, you can add your socials or your Discord.

</Step>
<Step>

### Submit for review

1. Check that the list on the left says **Ready to submit**.
2. Tick the box under your mod's name to agree to the Creator Terms and Content Rules. You only do this once.
3. Click **Submit for review**.

Verified creators see **Upload mod** instead. Their mods go live once the file check passes.

</Step>
<Step>

### Wait for the review

Your mod waits under **In review** in the Creator Panel. A moderator checks every mod before it goes live, and Divine sends you a notification when they're done.

### Start from Celestial instead

Celestial can start the draft for you. In **Creator Hub**, right-click the mod and click **Divine** under **Export**. Log in, fill in the name, champion, category and license, pick a thumbnail and click **Save as Draft**. Celestial packs the `.modpkg` and uploads it. Then click **Open in Divine** and finish the media, content labels and description on the website.

## The file check

Divine checks every file you upload. The result shows under the file:

<ParameterList
  parameters={[
    {
      name: "Waiting, Checking",
      description: "The check is running. Give it a minute.",
    },
    { name: "Passed", description: "Nothing to fix." },
    {
      name: "In review",
      description:
        "A moderator will look at this file before it goes live. Nothing to do yet.",
    },
    {
      name: "Needs changes",
      description:
        "The file was refused. The note under it says what to change.",
    },
  ]}
/>

A file is paused for a moderator only when it holds something a mod never needs, like a program or a script, or when the check can't read it. Notes under **Worth a look** never stop your mod. The one you'll see most:

- **Not repathed.** Some files still use the game's own paths, so your mod can clash with other mods for the same champion. [Repath it](/docs/lol/getting-started/repath-your-mod), export a new `.modpkg` and upload it again.

## Common problems

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="Divine won't take your file">

League uploads must be a `.modpkg`. A `.fantome`, `.zip` or `.rar` is refused. [Convert a .fantome](/docs/lol/mod-format/fantome), or export a `.modpkg` from Creator Hub. Over the size limit? Remove files your mod doesn't use and export again.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="You need to add a second file">

A League mod is one `.modpkg`. Put the extra version in a [layer](/docs/lol/mod-format/layers) and export again.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="You can't start a second mod">

New creators work on one mod at a time. Wait until a moderator publishes your first mod, then start the next one.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Your mod shows Changes requested">

Read the moderators' note at the top of your mod's form. Fix it, then click **Submit for review** again.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="You forgot something in the file while it's in review">

Open the mod in the Creator Panel and go to the **Releases** tab. You can replace, rename or remove its file there while it waits for review.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="You can't log in after you made your account">

Verify your email first. Open the email from Divine and click the link. Check your spam folder if you can't find it.

</Accordion>
</Accordions>
