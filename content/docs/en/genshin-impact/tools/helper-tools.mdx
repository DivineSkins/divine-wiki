You can make every Genshin Impact mod with XXMI Tools, gui_collect, and a texture editor. The smaller tools on this page each save time on one job. Learn the main tools first, so you can tell when a helper gets something wrong.

<Callout type="info" title="Community tools, untested">
  Every tool here is free and comes from a named community author. HoYoverse and
  the GIMI team do not make or support them. Nobody has tested them on Genshin
  Impact 7.2 for this wiki. Several of them edit your `.ini` files, so back up a
  mod before you run a tool on it.
</Callout>

## Blender helpers

These add-ons work next to XXMI Tools. Three more, Robust Weight Transfer, XXMI Buffer Export, and Genshin Face Modding Tools, are on the [XXMI Tools for Blender](/docs/genshin-impact/tools/blender-add-on) page.

  href="https://github.com/andyp123/blender_vertex_color_master"
  badge="Untested"
  icon={<Icon name="GitHub" />}

  Paints or fills one vertex color channel at a time, for example only the
  outline thickness. By andyp123.
</ToolCard>
<ToolCard
  name="Additional Batch Rename"
  href="https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/additional-batch-rename/"
  badge="Blender 4.4+"
  icon={<Icon name="Blender" />}
>
  Renames vertex groups, bones, and modifiers in bulk, with find and replace.
  Handy after a weight transfer. By Endertainer007.
</ToolCard>

- Vertex colors control shading and outlines. Keep the original's values where you are unsure, and write colors as hex, such as `FF8080`. [UV maps, vertex colors, and outlines](/docs/genshin-impact/models/uvs-and-vertex-colors) explains each channel.
- LEOAlphaPaint by HummyR also paints vertex colors. It dates from 2023 and has had no release since.

<ToolCard

  badge="Untested"
  icon={<Icon name="GitHub" />}

  A shared `.ini` that tells other mods the element of the character on screen,
  so a merged mod can switch by element. By ComplexSignal31.
</ToolCard>
<ToolCard
  name="RZMenu"
  href="https://github.com/rayvy/RZMenu"
  badge="Beta"
  icon={<Icon name="GitHub" />}
>
  A Blender add-on that builds in-game menus and toggles into your exported mod.
  Needs XXMI Tools 1.6.9 or newer. By rayvy.
</ToolCard>

Good to know:

- **SwapLock** and **Global Persist Swapkey** are explained on [Change a mod's keys and saved settings](/docs/genshin-impact/ini/change-mod-keys).
- **3Dmigoto Mods Merger** renames each original `.ini` to start with `DISABLED`. It switches between whole mods: it does not mix parts of different mods. [Combine mods into one merged mod](/docs/genshin-impact/ini/merged-mods) covers merging.
- **Element Detector** must know every character. Its generator was last updated for 6.2 characters, so check the repo date before you rely on it.
- **RZMenu** is a beta. Its author warns about crashes. Players need nothing extra: the export puts the menu into your mod.

## Port mods to another outfit

<ToolCard
  name="AG Remap (Anime Game Remap)"
  href="https://github.com/nhok0169/Anime-Game-Remap"
  badge="Untested"
  icon={<Icon name="GitHub" />}
>
  Moves a mod made for one outfit onto the same character's other outfit, with
  no Blender. About 29 Genshin Impact pairs. By Albert Gold.
</ToolCard>

- AG Remap pairs include Hu Tao and her Cherry outfit, Raiden and the Raiden boss, and Arlecchino and the Arlecchino boss. Version 5.0.0 is from October 2026.
- Each outfit is its own model with its own hashes. Never place a paid outfit over its free version.
- Ask a mod's author before you port, edit, or share their mod.

## Icons and loading screens

- **Icon Mod Creator** and **AvatarIconModPack** turn your pictures into icon mods. [Replace character icons and portraits](/docs/genshin-impact/ui/replace-icons) shows both.
- **Loading Screen Randomizer** by Arsinia turns a set of images into random loading screens, and replaces the white flash when the game starts. Get it from [GitHub](https://github.com/Arsinia/GI_LoadscreenRandomizer). Its last update was in September 2024, so it may not work on 7.2.

## Tools to skip

| Skip this                                                         | Why                                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Any `d3d11.dll` or "loader" that does not come from XXMI Launcher | Unofficial loaders are not supported, can break, and put your account at risk.      |
| HOYO-SHA, the hash auto-updater                                   | Closed source, its own license terms, and out of date.                              |
| Genshin Stella Mod                                                | A paid tier, and it bundles tools this wiki does not cover.                         |
| Tools that import other creators' finished mods back into Blender | Editing someone's mod needs their permission. Keep your own `.blend` files instead. |
| Tools that pull files out of the game                             | Not covered here. Use the asset repo or your own frame dump.                        |
