A few minutes after you log in with mods, Genshin Impact says "Illegal tool detected" and sends you back to the login screen. This page explains what the message means, what it means for your account, and what you can do. There is no supported fix.

## What you see

The game shows a pop-up titled `You have disconnected`, with a message like this:

```text
Illegal tool detected. Please restart your machine.
Error code: 10612-4001
```

The error code can differ. All of these belong to the same problem:

| Code         | Text that may come with it                            |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| `10612-4001` | "Illegal tool detected. Please restart your machine." |
| `15-4001`    | "Abnormal Data"                                       |
| `10310-4001` | "Abnormal activity detected on your device"           |
| `10010-4001` | Same pop-up, other code                               |
| No code      | Only "connection lost", seen since September 2025     |

It usually happens a few minutes after login, often around five minutes. You can log in again, and it comes back.

## What it means

Genshin Impact uses HoYoverse's kernel anti-cheat. It checks the game's memory for programs that should not be there. When it finds the mod loader that XXMI Launcher adds to the game, it disconnects you.

- **It is not a mod bug.** Your mods are not broken, and no mod setting causes it. The anti-cheat reacts to the mod loader itself.
- **It comes in waves.** Since early 2025, in waves, and in all regions since September 2025. It can stop for weeks and come back without a game update.
- **It differs from player to player.** The XXMI team says it depends on your region, your internet provider, your account, and the time in the update cycle. Two friends with the same setup can see different results.
- **It is a Genshin Impact problem.** The XXMI team said in 2026 that other HoYoverse games do not react to the loader this way. That can change at any time.

Nothing on your side makes it go away for good. The anti-cheat can learn to recognise any mod loader once enough players use it.

## What the mod tool makers say

- **The XXMI team will not ship a way around the anti-cheat.** They say any such method would start an arms race and put player accounts at risk of bans.
- **The GIMI team says the kick itself is not a ban.** Their news posts in 2025 described these errors as disconnects.
- **Single ban reports exist.** A few players have said they were banned after using XXMI. One report was a 24-hour ban while another third-party tool also ran. None of them is proven, and none can be ruled out.

## The risk

  use any tool that bypasses anti-cheat or gives a gameplay advantage.

The pop-up means the anti-cheat has seen the tool on your account. If you keep starting the game with mods after that, you are choosing to take that risk.

## Your choices

There is no supported fix. Tools that hide the mod loader from the anti-cheat put your account at risk and are not covered here.

These are the honest choices:

1. **Play without mods for now.** Close the game and start it from HoYoPlay or Epic Games, not from XXMI Launcher. Your mods stay in the `Mods` folder for later.
2. **Wait.** Detection has come and gone in waves since 2025. Your account carries the same risk when you return.
3. **Stop using mods on this account.** If losing this account would hurt, this is the only choice with no risk. Use mods only on an account you can afford to lose.

Keep XXMI Launcher's injection and run-method settings at their defaults. Community "anti-detection" tweaks are workarounds too.

## Fake fixes

During the September 2025 wave, files were uploaded as "fixes" for this error. Some of them carried malware. No download fixes this error.

Never run a file that promises to. Warning signs:

- It asks for admin rights.
- It comes in a password-protected archive.
- It comes from a brand-new account.
- It wants to replace `d3d11.dll`.

Get XXMI Launcher only from [its GitHub releases page](https://github.com/SpectrumQT/XXMI-Launcher/releases). It updates GIMI and its loader for you.
