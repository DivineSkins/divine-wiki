---
title: "Effects"
description: "Add glow, see-through parts and custom outline colors to your Genshin Impact mods with TexFx, the effects library built into GIMI."
category: "effects"
patch: "7.2"
---

Effects make parts of your own mod glow, go see-through, or get a new outline color. Keep the game as readable as the original: never remove or fade enemy effects, attack warnings or fog. Those are mods that give an unfair advantage.
