## What to put in your question

Helpers can fix a problem much faster when they have these details.

1. **What you see.** Copy the exact error text, or post a screenshot or a phone photo of the text on screen.
2. **Your versions.** XXMI Launcher shows them in its bottom-left corner, for example `LAUNCHER 2.5.0`, `XXMI 1.3.2`, and `GIMI 8.9.4`. Add your game version, such as 7.2.
3. **The banner.** Say whether `GIMI 8.9.4 F12 TO SHOW GUIDE` showed in the first 30 seconds of the game.
4. **What changed last.** A game update, a new mod, or a file you edited.
5. **Hidden errors.** If a mod fails with no message, untick **Mute Warnings** in the launcher's **GIMI** tab, press `F10` in game, and share the text that shows.

Creators: also post the full error text from Blender's System Console, your XXMI Tools and Blender versions, and the character you are working on.

Crop your UID out of every picture before you post it.

## Report a real bug

| Problem                                            | Where it goes                                                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| XXMI Launcher fails before the game's login screen | The XXMI Launcher issue tracker, with your `XXMI Launcher Log.txt`. [Report a launcher bug](/docs/genshin-impact/errors/launcher-problems#report-a-launcher-bug) has the steps. |
| A problem in game, or with one mod                 | Divine's Discord or the AGMG Discord, not the launcher tracker.                                                                                                                 |
| A bug in GIMI itself                               | The [GIMI-Package issue page](https://github.com/SilentNightSound/GIMI-Package/issues) on GitHub, or the AGMG Discord.                                                          |

### Spot an outdated guide

Genshin Impact modding changed a lot in 2025. A guide, video, or document is out of date if it:

- installs "standalone GIMI" or 3DMigoto by hand. Use XXMI Launcher instead.
- tells you to download ORFix or TexFx, or to put files in `Mods\BufferValues`. GIMI already ships these libraries.
- was written before October 22, 2025. Version 6.1 changed the texture and `.ini` rules most mods use. [Fix an old mod by hand](/docs/genshin-impact/ini/fix-an-old-mod) explains the change.
- uses `F1` for the in-game guide. The key is `F12`.

## Skip fake help

- **Lookalike sites.** XXMI Launcher comes only from `github.com/SpectrumQT/XXMI-Launcher`. Sites with "XXMI" in their name, such as `xxmilauncher.com`, are not official and host old copies.
- **Fake fixers.** A "fix" from a new account, in a password-protected archive, that asks for admin rights or replaces `d3d11.dll`, is a red flag. Fake fixers carrying malware have been reported.
- **Login requests.** No helper, mod, or tool needs your HoYoverse login.

[What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/genshin-impact/getting-started/stay-safe) explains how to check a download.
