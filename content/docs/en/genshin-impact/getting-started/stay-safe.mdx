## The risk, in short

<Callout type="danger" title="Mods can cost you your account">
  Mods break the Terms of Service of Genshin Impact. HoYoverse can suspend or
  permanently ban any account that uses third-party software, without warning or
  explanation. Genshin Impact's anti-cheat actively detects mod loaders. Bans of
  players who only used cosmetic mods have been reported. The risk is real and
  cannot be measured. Use mods only on an account you can afford to lose. Never
  use any tool that bypasses anti-cheat or gives a gameplay advantage.
</Callout>

## What HoYoverse's terms say

Genshin Impact runs under the HoYoPlay Terms of Service from COGNOSPHERE, HoYoverse's publishing company. Three parts matter for mods:

| Section   | What it says                                                                        |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2(b)(iv)  | You may not use or share programs that "may cause an unfair competitive advantage". |
| 2(b)(vii) | You may not copy, adapt or "otherwise create derivative works" from the game.       |
| 4(c)      | Any derivative work needs HoYoverse's "express prior written consent".              |

The terms also say HoYoverse can deny access "at any time for any reason without prior notice". HoYoverse has never said that cosmetic mods are allowed. The choice to use mods is yours.

## How Genshin Impact reacts to mods

Genshin Impact uses HoYoverse's kernel anti-cheat. It looks for the mod loader in memory. Since early 2025, in waves, and in all regions since September 2025, it has kicked modded players a few minutes after login.

You see a pop-up such as `Illegal tool detected` with an error code like `10612-4001`, `15-4001` or `10010-4001`. Sometimes it only says the connection was lost. The game then sends you back to the login screen.

- These kicks are disconnects. The GIMI team says the error itself is not a ban.
- Separate claims of bans from players who only used cosmetic mods exist. None is proven, and none can be ruled out.
- The XXMI team will not ship a way around the anti-cheat. They say any such fix would put accounts at risk.

There is no supported fix. Tools that hide the mod loader from the anti-cheat put your account at risk and are not covered here. If you see the error, stop and decide whether to keep using mods. [Illegal tool detected and 4001 errors](/docs/genshin-impact/errors/illegal-tool-detected) explains it in full.

Keep XXMI Launcher's injection and run-method settings at their defaults. Community "anti-detection" tweaks are workarounds too.

## Who sees your mods

Mods only change what your own PC draws. Players in your co-op world should see the normal game. The mod files stay on your PC, but the anti-cheat can still see the mod loader.

{/* NEEDS HANDS-ON TEST: two PCs in one co-op world, one modded, to confirm the other player sees the original model. */}

Your screenshots, videos and streams do show your mods, and often your UID next to them.

1. Hide or crop your UID before you share a modded picture or clip.
2. Never post modded footage in official channels: the official Discord, HoYoLAB, or official subreddits. A player of another HoYoverse game was banned from that game's official Discord for this.

Some mods hide the UID on screen. Use them for privacy only. They do not protect your account.

## Mods we never cover

These mods give you an advantage, or put your account and PC at extra risk. This wiki will never explain, link or show them.

| Type                        | Examples                                                                                               |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Treasure and puzzle markers | Lines or glowing pillars over chests, collectible pointers, puzzle helpers                             |
| Seeing more than you should | X-ray or see-through walls, fog removal                                                                |
| Hidden information          | In-game map overlays, ESP markers, buff and talent timers, counters, enemy health or weakness displays |
| Anti-cheat workarounds      | Anything that hides the mod loader from the anti-cheat or blocks it                                    |
| Other out-of-scope tools    | Private servers, FPS unlockers, account and wish tools, mobile "mods", NSFW content                    |

Ask one question before you install or share a mod: does it show me anything the normal game hides? If yes, it crosses the line.

## Download safely

### Know what a real mod holds

<img
  src="/wiki-images/genshin-impact-getting-started-stay-safe-1.webp"
  alt="Two example mod folders: a normal one with .ini, .ib, .buf and .dds files, and a bad one with an .exe, DLL files, a password-protected archive and a run-as-admin note"
/>

A Genshin Impact mod is a folder of `.ini` files, `.buf` and `.ib` model buffers, and `.dds` textures. Some also hold `.hlsl` shader files or a preview picture.

1. Open the archive with your archive tool before you extract it.
2. Delete the download if it holds an `.exe`, a `.dll`, or anything that asks for admin rights.
3. Check your browser's download list. Make sure the file you got is the archive you clicked, not a stray `.exe` from a fake download button.

### Watch out for fake fixers

Fake fixers carrying malware have been uploaded after game updates. Treat a "fix" as fake if it:

- comes from a new account with a single upload,
- sits in a password-protected archive,
- asks for admin rights,
- replaces `d3d11.dll`, the mod loader file.

Search results are also full of fake "mod menus" and "skin packs" on GitHub and elsewhere. They are tools for unfair play, or malware. Never download them.

### Keep your login to yourself

No mod or mod tool ever needs your HoYoverse account login. If one asks for it, close it and delete it.

## Respect other creators

- Credit everyone whose work you use.
- Ask the author before you edit, merge or reupload their mod.
- Never sell mods or put them behind a paywall. No HoYoverse text allows it.
