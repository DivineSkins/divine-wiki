Sound mods change voice lines, sound effects, or music. They work in a different way from every other mod on this wiki. This page explains the difference, the risks, and the tools. It is an overview, not a step-by-step guide.

<Callout type="warning" title="Sound mods change the game's own files">
  XXMI mods stay in their own folder and leave the game alone. Sound mods
  rewrite audio files inside the Genshin Impact game folder. That changes the
  game itself, which is a different kind of risk from XXMI mods, and nobody has
  measured it. Read [What's allowed and how to stay
  safe](/docs/genshin-impact/getting-started/stay-safe) first.
</Callout>

## How sound mods differ

<img
  src="/wiki-images/genshin-impact-more-mods-sound-mods-1.webp"
  alt="Two columns: XXMI mods live in the GIMI Mods folder and never change game files, while sound mods rewrite the game's .pck audio files and vanish after a game update or file check"
/>

- Genshin Impact keeps its sounds in Wwise audio packages: `.pck` files that hold `.wem` sounds.
- Sound tools rewrite those `.pck` files on disk. They do not use XXMI Launcher or GIMI.
- So `F6`, `F10`, and the `DISABLED_` folder name do nothing to sound mods.

## What updates and file checks do

- **A game update** replaces the changed audio files with new originals. Your sound mods are gone, and you must apply them again with your tool.
- **A file check**, when you repair the game in HoYoPlay or Epic Games, also puts the original files back. Repairing the game is a common crash fix, so expect to lose your sound mods when you do it.
- **Editing by hand** can damage a `.pck` file, one guide warns. Keep a copy of every file before you or a tool changes it.

## The tools

Written for Hoyo Audio Mod Maker 2.4 and XXAR 1.1.6. Nobody has tested these tools on Genshin Impact 7.2 for this wiki.

<ToolCard
  name="Hoyo Audio Mod Maker (HAMM)"
  href="https://github.com/NotThorny/Audio-Mod-Maker"
  badge="Untested"
  icon={<Icon name="GitHub" />}
>
  Makes and applies Genshin Impact sound mods. Downloads Wwise for you, picks
  voice lines by character, and can mute lines. Version 2.4, GPL-3.0.
</ToolCard>
<ToolCard
  name="XXAR (Hoyo Games Audio Replacer)"
  href="https://github.com/Entity378/XXAR"
  badge="Untested"
  icon={<Icon name="GitHub" />}
>
  A sound mod manager and creator. Packs Genshin Impact sound mods as `.giar`
  files. Version 1.1.6, GPL-3.0.
</ToolCard>

Good to know:

- **Wwise** is Audiokinetic's audio tool. It turns your sound files into `.wem`. It needs a free Audiokinetic account.
- **XXAR** is a fork of ZZAR by Pucas01. ZZAR covers Zenless Zone Zero only and has had no update since March 2026.
- **Older tools**, such as Sound Modding Scripts and the Genshin SoundMod Repacker from 2023 and 2024, are out of date.
