Every Deadlock mod uses the same few ideas and steps. Learn them once here, then use them for sounds, textures, icons, and models. No tools yet? Start with [Set up your creator tools](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/creator-setup).

## What do you want to do?

### Understand how mods load

- **Goal:** Learn what a VPK is, why file paths matter, and which mod wins.
- **Guide:** [How Deadlock mods work](/docs/deadlock/basics/how-mods-work)

---

### Find a hero's files

- **Goal:** Look up a hero's model, skeleton, portrait, and sound file paths.
- **Guide:** [Hero names and file paths](/docs/deadlock/basics/hero-codenames)

---

### Pack your mod

- **Goal:** Turn your compiled files into a `pakNN_dir.vpk` the game can load.
- **Guide:** [Pack your mod into a VPK](/docs/deadlock/basics/pack-a-vpk)

---

### Test your mod

- **Goal:** Load your mod on a private map and check every part of it.
- **Guide:** [Test your mod in game](/docs/deadlock/basics/test-your-mod)
