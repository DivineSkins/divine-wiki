Test every mod on your own PC before you share it. This page shows how to load your mod, start a private map, and what to check for each mod type. You need a packed VPK first: [Pack your mod into a VPK](/docs/deadlock/basics/pack-a-vpk).

## What you need

- Your mod, named like `pak05_dir.vpk`.
- Deadlock set up to load mods, with [Deadlock Mod Manager](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/install-with-mod-manager) or [by hand](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/install-by-hand).
- Optional: Source 2 Viewer, to compare paths when something does not show.

## Use one fixed pak number for test builds

Pick one number for your work in progress, such as `pak05_dir.vpk`. Overwrite that same file with every new build. Old test builds then never pile up in your addons folder.

1. Close Deadlock. The game reads mods only when it starts.
2. Copy your VPK into `game/citadel/addons/`. Replace the old test build.
3. Start Deadlock.

Restart the game after every new build. No source confirms that the game reloads a changed VPK while it runs. {/* NEEDS HANDS-ON TEST: whether Deadlock picks up a replaced addon VPK without a restart */}

Two more tips:

- Turn off your other mods while you test. A mod with a lower number can hide yours.
- If you test through Deadlock Mod Manager, zip your VPK first. Put the `.vpk` at the top level of the zip, not in a folder. {/* NEEDS HANDS-ON TEST: how to add a local zip in the current Deadlock Mod Manager, and the button label */}

## Load a private map

These maps run on your own PC, not on Valve's servers.

1. Press **F7** to open the console.
2. Type one of these commands and press **Enter**:
   - `map dl_hideout` loads the hideout.
   - `map new_player_basics` loads the new player basics map.
   - `map dl_midtown` loads the full main map.
3. Pick your hero.
4. To pick another hero, type `changeteam 0`. The team and hero choice opens again.

## What to check

Check every part of your mod, not only the part you changed.

<Tabs items={["Skins and models", "Sounds", "Icons and UI", "Particles"]}>

<Tab value="Skins and models">

1. Walk around and look at your hero from every side.
2. Aim down sights. The camera should sit where it does on the normal hero, not in the middle of the screen.
3. Use every ability. Some abilities switch parts of the model on or off.
4. Die once and look at the body.
5. Check the health bar. It should float above the hero, not sit at the feet.
6. Check the hero select screen and the shop screen.
7. Look for missing or wrong textures on every part of the model.

</Tab>

<Tab value="Sounds">

1. Open the console and type `snd_showstart 1`. The console now prints each sound path as it starts.
2. Make the sound happen in game, for example by using the ability.
3. Check that your sound plays and its path shows in the console.
4. Compare the volume and length with the original sound. Keep them close to the original.

</Tab>

<Tab value="Icons and UI">

1. Find every place your image appears. A hero has six portraits. Check the hero card, the small icon, the minimap icon, and the tall portrait. Also check the low health and kill streak portraits.
2. Check that each image is sharp and not stretched.
3. Check that see-through parts stay see-through, like in the original.

</Tab>

<Tab value="Particles">

1. Use the ability with your effect.
2. While it plays, type `dumpparticlelist` in the console. It lists the particle files in use.
3. Check that your color shows on every part of the effect. Some effects use several particle files.
4. Make sure the effect is as easy to see as the original.

{/* NEEDS HANDS-ON TEST: whether dumpparticlelist works on a normal private map or needs a special server flag */}

</Tab>

</Tabs>

Remember the cosmetic rule. A skin, sound, or effect must not make enemies easier to see or hear. [What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe) lists what crosses the line.

## Useful console commands

| Command            | What it does                                     |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| `map dl_hideout`   | Loads the hideout on your PC.                    |
| `changeteam 0`     | Opens the team and hero choice again.            |
| `snd_showstart 1`  | Prints each sound path as the sound starts.      |
| `soundinfo`        | Lists the sounds playing right now.              |
| `play <path>`      | Plays one sound file, such as `play sounds/...`. |
| `dumpparticlelist` | Lists the particle effects in use.               |

These commands come from community notes written before the "City Never Sleeps" update. ## Read errors in the console

The console also shows errors and warnings while the game loads your files.

1. Load a private map with your mod in place.
2. Open the console with **F7**.
3. Read any errors or warnings that name your files.

One known example: a model with broken camera attachments fills the console with green text. The text names the bad attachment.

Does your mod not show at all? Then the cause is almost always the file name, the folder, or a path. Check them with [Pack your mod into a VPK](/docs/deadlock/basics/pack-a-vpk).

## Before you queue again

<Callout type="warning" title="Clean up before a real match">
  Valve's game refuses to start matchmaking while any party member runs tools
  mode. Close your tools first.
</Callout>

1. Close CSDK 12 and every other modding tool.
2. Remove any custom map VPKs you tested. Custom maps do not work in Valve matches.
3. Check that you changed only the `SearchPaths` part of `gameinfo.gi`. Edits to other parts block matchmaking.
4. Restart Deadlock, so nothing from your test session carries over. {/* NEEDS HANDS-ON TEST: whether a test session (private map, console commands) has any effect on queueing without a restart (gap G07) */}
