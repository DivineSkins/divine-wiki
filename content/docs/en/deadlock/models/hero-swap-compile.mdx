## Why CSDK 12 cannot finish the job

Almost every hero uses AnimGraph 2. Its animations live in separate files, driven by a skeleton file (`.vnmskel`) and graph files (`.vnmgraph`). Your model must point to the hero's own skeleton and graphs. Without them, the hero stands in an A-pose and does not turn.

CSDK 12 cannot compile these links. Valve's CS2 Workshop Tools can. You use its ModelDoc for this one compile and take only the finished model back. Your materials stay in CSDK 12.

## Compile in CS2 and pack

<Steps>
<Step>

### Copy your model to CS2 Workshop Tools

1. Close CSDK 12.
2. Start CS2 Workshop Tools and create a new addon. Give it the same name as your CSDK 12 addon.
3. Open the new addon's content folder, `content/csgo_addons/<addon>/`, inside your Counter-Strike 2 folder.
4. Copy the `models` folder from `Reduced_CSDK_12/content/citadel_addons/<addon>/` into it. Keep the same folder structure.

The copy holds `viper_work.vmdl`, your DMX files, and your material sources. The DMX files must sit in this addon. ModelDoc cannot load meshes from another addon.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add the skeleton and graph references

1. In the CS2 Asset Browser, find `viper_work.vmdl` and open it in ModelDoc.
2. Ignore the error and the red checkerboard materials. CS2's shaders differ from Deadlock's.
3. If ModelDoc reports a bad content path for animations, disable `AnimationList`.
4. In Source 2 Viewer, open the original hero's `.vmdl_c`. Go to the **Data** tab and scroll to the bottom.
5. Note the skeleton path and the two graph paths there.
6. In ModelDoc, choose <MenuPath path="Add > NmSkeletonReference" />. When a file picker opens, close it and choose **Keep**.
7. Paste the skeleton path into the new node.
8. Choose <MenuPath path="Add > DefaultAnimGraph2" /> and paste the main graph path.
9. Choose <MenuPath path="Add > AnimGraph2" />. Name it `ui`, in lowercase, and paste the UI graph path.
10. Click **Compile**.

For Vyper, the three values look like this:

```text
NmSkeletonReference   models/heroes_staging/viper/viper.vnmskel
DefaultAnimGraph2     animgraphs/animgraph2/hero/hero.vnmgraph+viper.vnmgraph
AnimGraph2 (ui)       animgraphs/animgraph2/hero/hero_ui.vnmgraph+viper.vnmgraph
```

Other heroes follow the same pattern, but not always with the same name. Kelvin and Viscous share one skeleton, and Ivy's skeleton sits under her old name `tengu`. Always copy the paths from the Data tab.

</Step>
<Step>

### Take only the compiled model

1. In the CS2 Asset Browser, right-click your model and open the folder of its compiled file.
2. Find `viper_work.vmdl_c` in `game/csgo_addons/<addon>/models/heroes_staging/viper/`.
3. Copy that one file. Take nothing else from this folder.

Need to change something later? Make the change in CSDK 12, copy the changed nodes into the CS2 copy, and compile there again.

</Step>
<Step>

### Build the staging folder

<img
  src="/wiki-images/deadlock-models-hero-swap-compile-4.webp"
  alt="Diagram: materials and textures come from the CSDK 12 game folder, the model comes only from the CS2 Workshop Tools game folder, both go into a staging folder at the original paths, which is packed into one pakNN_dir.vpk"
/>

1. Make a new folder for your mod, such as `myskin_pack`.
2. Copy the `models` folder from CSDK 12's `game/citadel_addons/<addon>/` into it. It holds your compiled materials and textures.
3. Delete the CSDK 12 build of `viper_work.vmdl_c` from the copy.
4. Paste the CS2 build of `viper_work.vmdl_c` in its place.
5. Rename it to the original name, `viper.vmdl_c`.
6. Remove every file the mod must not ship. The list is below.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/deadlock-models-hero-swap-compile-5.webp"
  alt="Folder tree: myskin_pack/models/heroes_staging/viper/ holds viper.vmdl_c from CS2 and a materials folder from CSDK 12. Never ship viper.vnmskel_c, clips, scripts, dmx or vmdl source files, or the CSDK 12 build viper_work.vmdl_c."
/>

Never ship:

- the hero's `.vnmskel_c` skeleton;
- `.vnmclip_c` animation clips;
- anything in `scripts/`, such as `.vdata_c` files;
- compiled files you did not change.

These files break other mods for the same hero, and they break after game updates.

</Step>
<Step>

### Pack and test

1. Pack the staging folder into one VPK. Follow [Pack your mod into a VPK](/docs/deadlock/basics/pack-a-vpk).
2. Name it like `pak05_dir.vpk` and put it in `game/citadel/addons/`.
3. Close CSDK 12 and CS2 Workshop Tools.
4. Start Deadlock and load a private map. [Test your mod in game](/docs/deadlock/basics/test-your-mod) shows how.

Then check every item:

- Walk and run. The hero animates and turns with the camera.
- Aim down sights. The camera sits behind the hero like on the normal hero, and the zoom matches.
- Use every ability. Parts that switch on or off still do.
- Die once. The ragdoll falls without stretching.
- Look at the health bar. It floats above the head.
- Listen for footsteps and ability sounds.
- Look at the hero select and shop screens.
- Walk far away from the hero. The LODs keep the same shape.

{/* SCREENSHOT: deadlock-models-hero-swap-compile-6.webp, an original test skin on Vyper running in the hideout, camera behind the shoulder and health bar above the head */}

Something looks wrong? [Model and skin problems](/docs/deadlock/errors/model-problems) lists each symptom and the part that fixes it. Before you queue for a real match, follow [Before you queue again](/docs/deadlock/basics/test-your-mod#before-you-queue-again).

</Step>
</Steps>

## Heroes that may still use AnimGraph 1

Violet and Nurse Harrow had no main AnimGraph 2 graph in build 6779. If your hero's Data tab shows no `hero.vnmgraph+` path, it may still use the older AnimGraph 1.

Fuji's guide handles AnimGraph 1 heroes with one node. Add an `AnimIncludeModel` node. Point it to your "export as is" copy of the original model, written with a `.vmdl` ending. Without it, the hero T-poses and does not turn. This comes from one guide and may differ on the current build.
