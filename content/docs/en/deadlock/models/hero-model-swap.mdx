A hero model swap puts your own character on a Deadlock hero. It is the biggest mod you can make: four parts, four tools, and a lot of checking. This page shows the whole path and the rules. Do [your first model swap](/docs/deadlock/models/prop-swap) before you start.

## What a hero swap is

You rig your mesh to the hero's own skeleton and compile it at the hero's original path. The game still plays the hero's animations, abilities, and camera. Only the look changes.

Like every mod, it only changes your game. You see your skin on every player who picks that hero, enemies included. Other players see the normal hero.

## Before you start

- Finish the [soul container swap](/docs/deadlock/models/prop-swap). It teaches ModelDoc and the Material Editor on a small model.
- Learn the [Blender import](/docs/deadlock/models/import-into-blender).
- Plan a dry run. Pugzilla runs all four parts once with the unchanged hero mesh, then swaps in the new mesh. It proves your setup works before you put work into your own model.
- Pick an easy hero. Pugzilla learns on Vyper, whose model has few special parts. The video also notes two special cases. Pocket and Haze keep some animations in their model for particles. Haze also needs extra material groups for invisibility.
- Use your own design. Characters ported from other games, films, or anime carry copyright risk. See [Credits, licenses, and permissions](/docs/deadlock/publishing/credits-and-licenses).

On build 6779, Violet and Nurse Harrow have no AnimGraph 2 graph of their own. Part 4 may not work for them, so pick another hero.

### Tools

| Tool                                                                    | Used in              | What for                                 |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| [Source 2 Viewer](/docs/deadlock/tools/source-2-viewer), 20.0 or newer  | Start, parts 3 and 4 | Export the hero and read original values |
| [Blender and BlenderSource2Tools](/docs/deadlock/tools/blender-add-ons) | Part 1               | Rig your mesh and export DMX             |
| [CSDK 12](/docs/deadlock/tools/csdk-12): ModelDoc and Material Editor   | Parts 2, 3           | Build the model and its materials        |
| [CS2 Workshop Tools](/docs/deadlock/tools/cs2-workshop-tools), free     | Part 4               | Add the AnimGraph 2 links and compile    |
| A text editor, such as Notepad++                                        | Parts 2 to 4         | Edit the `.vmdl` file by hand            |

Use Source 2 Viewer 20.0 or newer. Older builds decompile the camera attachments slightly wrong, which puts your camera in the middle of the screen.

## The four parts

<img
  src="/wiki-images/deadlock-models-hero-model-swap-2.webp"
  alt="Diagram: export the hero from Source 2 Viewer, then part 1 in Blender makes DMX meshes, part 2 in ModelDoc builds the model and materials, part 3 copies attachments and game data, part 4 in CS2 tools compiles the vmdl_c, and the result is packed into one VPK"
/>

| Part                                                                                 | What you do                                                                | What you get                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| 1. [Rig your model in Blender](/docs/deadlock/models/hero-swap-blender)              | Export the hero, fit your mesh to its skeleton, and transfer the weights   | `.dmx` mesh files                       |
| 2. [Build the model in ModelDoc](/docs/deadlock/models/hero-swap-modeldoc)           | Swap the meshes, then rebuild bodygroups, materials, the ragdoll, and LODs | A `.vmdl` that compiles, plus materials |
| 3. [Attachments, camera, and game data](/docs/deadlock/models/hero-swap-attachments) | Copy the original's attachments, camera previews, and game data blocks     | A `.vmdl` with a working camera         |
| 4. [Add AnimGraph 2 and compile](/docs/deadlock/models/hero-swap-compile)            | Add the skeleton and animation graph links, compile, and pack              | The hero's `.vmdl_c` in your VPK        |

Part 4 needs a second tool kit. Almost every hero uses AnimGraph 2: its animations live in separate files, and the model must link to them. CSDK 12 cannot compile those links, so the last compile happens in CS2 Workshop Tools. Without them, the hero stands in an A-pose or does not turn.

## Rules you must keep

These rules keep your swap cosmetic and stop it from breaking.

- **Keep the skeleton.** Never move, rename, or delete the hero's bones. You may add new bones for cloth.
- **Keep the hitboxes.** Leave the hitbox sets exactly as they are.
- **Copy every attachment and game data block.** They place the camera, the gun's aim point, the health bar, and some hero sounds.
- **Never change camera values.** Camera offsets and field of view come from the original. Changed values give you a wider or higher view than other players.
- **Ship only what you made.** Your `.vmdl_c`, `.vmat_c`, and `.vtex_c` files. Never ship the hero's `.vnmskel_c` skeleton, its `.vnmclip_c` animation clips, or anything from `scripts/`. They break other mods and break after updates.

<Callout type="danger" title="Size and shape are part of the fair-play line">
  A smaller, thinner, or taller model gives an advantage. It changes how easy
  the hero is to see and aim at. Match the original proportions. Do not make any
  part glow, turn see-through, or go very dark. Read [What's allowed and how to
  stay safe](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe).
</Callout>

## Variations

- **Only new colors or textures?** You need no model work. Use [Recolor with material values](/docs/deadlock/textures/recolor-a-material) or [Retexture a hero](/docs/deadlock/textures/retexture).
- **Add an accessory**, like a hat or a bag, to the stock hero. Import the stock hero in Blender, add your mesh, and weight it to the nearest bone. Then export the stock meshes and your accessory as DMX and continue with parts 2 to 4.
- **Give a hero another hero's look.** You still build the target hero's model. Export the other hero as glTF, delete its armature, and fit its mesh to the target's skeleton like any custom mesh. Do not count on copying one hero's compiled model to another hero's path. Each model links to its own skeleton and animations.

## When something breaks

| Symptom                                       | Where to fix it                                                                 |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ModelDoc crashes when you open the hero       | [Part 2](/docs/deadlock/models/hero-swap-modeldoc)                              |
| Strange bends when the hero moves             | [Part 1](/docs/deadlock/models/hero-swap-blender), weight painting              |
| The camera sits in the middle of the screen   | [Part 3](/docs/deadlock/models/hero-swap-attachments)                           |
| The health bar sits at the feet               | [Part 3](/docs/deadlock/models/hero-swap-attachments)                           |
| The hero stands in an A-pose or does not turn | [Part 4](/docs/deadlock/models/hero-swap-compile)                               |
| It broke after a game update                  | [Update your mod after a patch](/docs/deadlock/publishing/update-after-a-patch) |
