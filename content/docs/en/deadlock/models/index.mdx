Model swaps change the shape of a prop or a hero, not only its colors. New to models? Start with the soul container. It teaches the tools on a small model. Keep every model the same size and shape as the original. Read [What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe) before you start.

## What do you want to do?

### Make your first model swap

- **Goal:** Replace the soul container with your own 3D model.
- **Method:** Fit your model to its one bone in Blender, swap the mesh in ModelDoc, and add one material.
- **Guide:** [Your first model swap: a soul container](/docs/deadlock/models/prop-swap)

---

### Open a Deadlock model in Blender

- **Goal:** Get a hero or prop into Blender to edit it or use it as a reference.
- **Guide:** [Import a Deadlock model into Blender](/docs/deadlock/models/import-into-blender)

---

### Replace a hero's model

- **Goal:** Put your own character on a hero.
- **Method:** Four parts: Blender, ModelDoc, attachments, and a final compile.
- **Guide:** Read [Hero model swap: overview](/docs/deadlock/models/hero-model-swap) first.

---

### Add cloth

- **Goal:** Make hair, coats, or capes move on your model.
- **Guide:** [Add cloth to a model](/docs/deadlock/models/cloth)

### Change menu poses

- **Goal:** Change how your skin stands on the hero select, shop, and end screens.
