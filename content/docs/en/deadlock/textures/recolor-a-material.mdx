<LevelPill level="beginner" />

Recolor part of a hero by changing material values in CSDK 12. You do not paint any images. In this guide you turn Pocket's briefcase into shiny gold. Set up your tools first with [Set up your creator tools](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/creator-setup).

## What you need

- Deadlock, installed and up to date.
- Source 2 Viewer and CSDK 12. Install them with [Set up your creator tools](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/creator-setup).
- The hero's codename. Pocket's briefcase uses `synth`. Other heroes are in [Hero names and file paths](/docs/deadlock/basics/hero-codenames).

## What a material is

A material is a small file that tells the game how a surface looks. It names a shader, lists the texture images, and holds values like color, roughness, and metalness. Hero materials use the `pbr.vfx` shader.

The game reads compiled materials, which end in `.vmat_c`. You decompile one, change its values in the Material Editor, and compile it again.

## Keep it fair

A recolor changes that hero for you everywhere, enemies included. Nobody else sees it. So a bright skin can make an enemy easier to spot.

<Callout
  type="danger"
  title="No glowing, bright, see-through, or very dark skins"
>
  Do not turn on self-illumination or make a hero glow. Do not make a hero much
  brighter, near-transparent, or very dark. These change how easy a hero is to
  see. Read [What's allowed and how to stay
  safe](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe).
</Callout>

## Quick options

These need no CSDK 12. They give you less control than the full steps.

- **Mod Foundry in Deadlock Mod Manager.** Open **Settings > Experimental > Mod Foundry**. It recolors a hero's body and weapon and exports a VPK. See the [Mod Foundry docs](https://docs.deadlockmods.app/using-mod-manager/customization).
- **DeadMod in your browser.** Export a texture image with Source 2 Viewer and recolor it in an image editor. Then [DeadMod](https://redmser.github.io/DeadMod/) builds a VPK from your edited image. This edits images, not material values.

## Find the material

1. Open Source 2 Viewer.
2. Open `Deadlock/game/citadel/pak01_dir.vpk`.
3. Go to `models/heroes_staging/synth/materials/`.
4. Find `pocket_suitcase.vmat_c`.

Materials do not always sit next to the model. Pocket's model is in `models/heroes_wip/pocket/`, but the briefcase material is in the `synth` folder. For other heroes, open the hero's model and check its **References** tab for the real material paths.

## Decompile it into your addon

1. Open the CSDK 12 launcher and create a new addon. Name it in lowercase with no spaces, for example `goldcase`.
2. Right-click the addon and choose **Explore Content Folder**.
3. Inside it, rebuild the material's path: `models/heroes_staging/synth/materials/`.
4. In Source 2 Viewer, right-click `pocket_suitcase.vmat_c` and choose **Decompile & export**.
5. Save it into the folder you made in step 3.

{/* NEEDS HANDS-ON TEST: whether Decompile & export also writes the material's PNG inputs, and where it puts them (flat in the chosen folder or sorted by path). */}

## Open it in the Material Editor

1. In the CSDK 12 launcher, select `goldcase` and click **Launch Tools**. The Asset Browser opens.
2. Set the Asset Browser to show only your addon's files.
3. Type `type=material_asset` in the search box to show only materials.
4. Double-click `pocket_suitcase` to open it in the Material Editor.
5. Check that the shader at the top is `pbr.vfx`. A decompiled hero material should already use it.
6. Set the preview model to Pocket: `models/heroes_wip/pocket/pocket.vmdl`. Now you see the briefcase on the hero.

## Change the values

Each setting can read from a texture image or use a fixed value. For polished gold, change three settings:

1. Change **Roughness** from texture to **Color**. Pick pure black. This makes the surface smooth and shiny.
2. Change **Metalness** from texture to **Slider**. Set it to 1.0. This makes the surface look like metal.
3. Change **Color** to the gold you want. The example uses 255 192 47.
4. Check the preview. Adjust the values until you like the result.
5. Press Ctrl+S to save. Close the Material Editor.

<Callout type="info" title="Tint instead of replace">
  Materials also have a color tint value (`g_vColorTint1` in the file). It
  multiplies the texture's color, so details stay visible. Many hero materials
  have a tint mask, so the tint only shows on some parts.
</Callout>

## Compile and collect the files

1. In the Asset Browser, right-click the material and choose **Recompile > Full**.
2. Right-click `goldcase` in the CSDK 12 launcher and choose **Explore Game Folder**.
3. Open `models/heroes_staging/synth/materials/`. You see the new `pocket_suitcase.vmat_c` and new `.vtex_c` texture files.
4. Make a new folder for your mod, for example `goldcase_pack`.
5. Inside it, rebuild the same path: `models/heroes_staging/synth/materials/`.
6. Copy the new `.vmat_c` and every new `.vtex_c` into it.

The new material points to its textures by their compiled names. Those names include a code, like `abrams_coat_color_png_63291cfb.vtex_c`. If you ship the material without its new textures, the game cannot find them.

## Common problems

- **Nothing changes in game.** You packed the `.vmat` source file, or you forgot to recompile. Pack the compiled `.vmat_c` from the game folder.
- **The old look stays.** Your folder path is different from the original. Check every folder name against Source 2 Viewer.
- **Purple checkerboard in the Material Editor.** A texture slot lost its image. Click the magnifier on that slot and pick the image that came with the material.
- **Checkerboard or missing texture in game.** The new `.vtex_c` files are missing from your VPK. Copy them next to the `.vmat_c` and pack again.
- **It worked before an update.** Game updates can move hero files. Find the material again and check the path.
