A recolor changes a hero for you everywhere, enemies included. Never make a hero glow, go bright, near-transparent, or very dark. Read [What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe) before you start.

## What do you want to do?

### Recolor part of a hero

- **Goal:** Change colors, shine, or metal look without painting.
- **Method:** Edit material values in CSDK 12's Material Editor and compile.
- **Guide:** [Recolor with material values](/docs/deadlock/textures/recolor-a-material)
