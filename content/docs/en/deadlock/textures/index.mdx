You see a recolor on every copy of that hero, enemies included. Never make a hero glow, go bright, near-transparent, or very dark. Read [What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe) before you start.

## What do you want to do?

- [Recolor with material values](/docs/deadlock/textures/recolor-a-material): change colors, shine, or metal look in the Material Editor, without painting.
- [Retexture a hero](/docs/deadlock/textures/retexture): paint your own details, patterns, or colors on a hero's images and rebuild the material.
- [Materials for custom models](/docs/deadlock/textures/materials-for-custom-models): give your own model the shader settings Deadlock heroes need.
- [Hero material settings reference](/docs/deadlock/textures/material-reference): look up what each setting and texture slot in the hero shader does.
