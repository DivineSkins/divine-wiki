Divine is where you share your mod with players. This page walks you through every screen of the upload, from a new account to a mod in review. [Pack your mod into a VPK](/docs/deadlock/basics/pack-a-vpk) and [test it in game](/docs/deadlock/basics/test-your-mod) first. If it uses anyone else's work, read [Credits, licenses, and permissions](/docs/deadlock/publishing/credits-and-licenses) before you start.

## Before you upload

Do these checks before you submit.

1. Test the mod in game with no other mods installed.
2. Check that it gives no gameplay advantage. Camp timers, visibility skins, and louder ability sounds are out. [What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe) has the full list.
3. Check that you have permission for every model, texture, sound, and image you did not make.
4. Keep the title, description, thumbnail, and screenshots safe for work. Only the mod's files can have 18+ content, and then you must mark the mod 18+.
5. Note where AI made or helped make any part of the mod or its page. The upload form asks about it.
6. For skins: check that the hero animates and the camera works. Fix any T-pose, missing attachments, or bad distortion.
7. Keep it free. Every mod on Divine is free, so never sell your mod or lock it behind a paywall.

## Build your archive

Divine takes Deadlock mods as a `.zip` or `.7z` archive, up to 300 MB. A bare `.vpk` file is not accepted. Upload only what players need: your VPK and a short readme.

1. Make a new folder for the upload.
2. Copy your `pakNN_dir.vpk` into it.
3. Add a `readme.txt`. Say which pak number to use, which mods it conflicts with, and how to install it by hand.
4. Pack the folder as a `.zip` or `.7z` file. Do not set a password.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/deadlock-publishing-publish-on-divine-1.webp"
  alt="An upload archive holding only pak05_dir.vpk and readme.txt, next to a list of things to leave out: programs, source files, extra game files, and passwords"
/>

A mod never needs a program. Leave out `.exe`, `.dll`, and `.bat` files. Divine also refuses `.rar` files and archives inside archives.

If your mod has more than one VPK, put them all in the archive, or upload each part as its own file. Tell players how to install every part by hand.

## Take good screenshots

1. Take at least two screenshots in game. Show the front and the back of the hero. Divine won't send a Deadlock mod for review with fewer than two.
2. Add a close-up of a detail, or an ability your mod changes.
3. Use renders from Blender or other tools only as extras, never instead of in-game shots.
4. For a sound mod, upload a short in-game clip to YouTube. You paste the link in the upload form.

The showcase guide in Next steps explains how to take clean shots.

## Upload it on Divine

Each picture below shows the screen for that step. The purple rings mark what to click.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create your account

Skip this step if you already have an account. Log in instead.

1. Open [divineskins.gg](https://divineskins.gg).
2. Click **Log in** at the top right.
3. Click **Create one here**.
4. Enter a username, your email, and a password.
5. Wait for the security check to show **Success!**.
6. Click **Create account**.
7. Open the email from Divine and click the link in it. This verifies your email. You can't log in until you do.

You can also click the Google or Discord button on the Log in page. That makes your account in one click.

/>

</Step>
<Step>

### Open the Creator Panel

The Creator Panel is where you upload and manage your mods.

1. Click your profile picture at the top right.
2. Click **Creator panel**.

You can also open [divineskins.gg/creator-panel](https://divineskins.gg/creator-panel) directly.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/deadlock-publishing-publish-on-divine-3.webp"
  width={380}
  alt="The account menu open under the profile picture, with the profile picture marked 1 and Creator panel marked 2"
/>

</Step>
<Step>

### Start a new mod

1. Click **Create mod**.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/deadlock-publishing-publish-on-divine-4.webp"
  width={340}
  alt="The Creator Panel card for a new account, with 0 published mods, 0 drafts, and the Create mod button ringed"
/>

2. Under **Game**, pick **Deadlock**.
3. Type a name that says what the mod is, like "Gold trim for Pocket's briefcase". Skip names like "My mod". You can change it later.
4. Click **Create draft**. The upload form opens.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/deadlock-publishing-publish-on-divine-5.webp"
  width={520}
  alt="The New mod window with Game set to Deadlock marked 1, Mod name filled in marked 2, and the Create draft button marked 3"
/>

The form has five steps in a list on the left. Basic info, Files, Media, and Content labels are required. Description is optional, but players read it. Divine saves each step as you go. Click **Save and exit** to stop and come back later from **Drafts** in the Creator Panel.

<Callout type="info" title="Your first mod">
  New creators work on one draft at a time. After you submit it, you can start
  your next mod once a moderator publishes the first one. After that, you can
  keep up to 5 drafts.
</Callout>

</Step>
<Step>

### Fill in Basic info

1. Pick a **Category**, like Hero Skins, Sounds & Voice, or HUD & UI.
2. For a hero skin, pick the **Hero**. Hero Skins mods need one.
3. Pick a **Theme** and a **Feature** if one fits. Add a few **Tags**: type each one and press Enter.
4. Pick a **License**. CC BY-NC 4.0 is set for you. The credits guide linked at the top helps you choose.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/deadlock-publishing-publish-on-divine-6.webp"
  alt="The Basic info step with Category set to Hero Skins marked 1, Hero set to Pocket marked 2, and License set to CC BY-NC 4.0 marked 3. Theme is Recolor, Feature is Textures, and the tags are gold and briefcase"
/>

</Step>
<Step>

### Upload your archive

1. Click **Next: Files**.
2. Drop your `.zip` or `.7z` on the box, or click the box to pick it.
3. Keep **Main** as the file name, or type a short name for this file.
4. Click **Upload file**.
5. Wait until the file shows under **Uploaded** with a green check.

Your mod has more than one part? Add each part with **Add another file**.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/deadlock-publishing-publish-on-divine-7.webp"
  alt="Two views of the Files step. Top: pocket_gold_trim.zip picked, the file name Main, and the Upload file button ringed. Bottom: the file listed under Uploaded with a green check, .zip, 4.1 MB"
/>

</Step>
<Step>

### Add a thumbnail and screenshots

1. Click **Next: Media**.
2. Under **Thumbnail**, add one image of this mod. Use 16:9, at least 960×540 pixels.
3. Under **Gallery**, click **Add images**. Add at least two in-game screenshots.
4. Drag the screenshots to change their order.
5. Optional: paste a YouTube link to a short in-game clip.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/deadlock-publishing-publish-on-divine-8.webp"
  alt="The Media step with the thumbnail marked 1 and two gallery screenshots marked 2. The YouTube video box is empty"
/>

</Step>
<Step>

### Answer the content questions

1. Click **Next: Content labels**.
2. **Is this mod NSFW (18+)?** Answer **No** for most mods. If the mod's files have nudity, graphic gore, or other 18+ content, answer **Yes** and pick every category that fits. Mods for Graves and Rem can't be 18+.
3. **Did AI make or help make any part of this mod or its page?** Answer **No** if you made everything without AI. If AI helped, answer **Yes** and set each part to the level that fits.

Not sure? The [18+ rules](https://divineskins.gg/legal/content-rules#adult) and the [AI rules](https://divineskins.gg/legal/content-rules#generative-ai) explain each answer.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/deadlock-publishing-publish-on-divine-9.webp"
  alt="The Content labels step with No picked for Is this mod NSFW (18+)? marked 1, and No picked for the AI question marked 2"
/>

</Step>
<Step>

### Write a description

1. Click **Next: Description**.
2. Click **Write description**.
3. Say what the mod changes. Add how to install it, which pak number to use, and what it conflicts with.
4. Credit yourself, every co-author, and every creator whose work is in your mod.
5. Click **Save**.

The editor shows a preview on the right. **Template** fills in a starting layout. Under **Links**, you can add your socials or your Discord.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/deadlock-publishing-publish-on-divine-10.webp"
  alt="The Description editor with Markdown on the left, the preview on the right, and the Save button ringed. The text covers what the mod changes, an Install section, and a Conflicts section"
/>

</Step>
<Step>

### Submit for review

1. Check that the list on the left says **Ready to submit** and **4/4**.
2. Tick the box under your mod's name to agree to the Creator Terms and Content Rules. You only do this once.
3. Click **Submit for review**.

Verified creators see **Upload mod** instead. Their mods go live once the file check passes.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/deadlock-publishing-publish-on-divine-11.webp"
  alt="The top of the upload form with the agreement box ticked and marked 1, the Submit for review button marked 2, and the steps list showing Ready to submit 4/4"
/>

</Step>
<Step>

### Wait for the review

Divine shows **Sent for review** and takes you back to the Creator Panel. Your mod waits under **In review**. A moderator checks every mod before it goes live, and Divine sends you a notification when they're done.

## Common problems

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="Divine won't take your file">

Check the file type. Divine takes a `.zip` or `.7z` up to 300 MB, with `.vpk` files inside. Pack a bare `.vpk` into a `.zip` first. Remove any `.rar` file, archive inside the archive, or program.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Submit for review stays grey">

Look at the list on the left. Every required step needs a green check. The most common miss is the second gallery screenshot.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="You can't start a second mod">

New creators work on one mod at a time. Wait until a moderator publishes your first mod, then start the next one.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Your mod shows Changes requested">

Read the moderators' note at the top of your mod's form. Fix it, then click **Submit for review** again.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="You can't log in after you made your account">

Verify your email first. Open the email from Divine and click the link. Check your spam folder if you can't find it.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Your skin T-poses or the camera is wrong">

See [Model and skin problems](/docs/deadlock/errors/model-problems).

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Your mod uses someone else's work">

Ask the creator for permission, save their answer, and credit them. The credits guide linked at the top explains how.

</Accordion>
</Accordions>
