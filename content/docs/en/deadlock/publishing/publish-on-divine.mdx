Divine is where you share your mod with players. [Pack your mod into a VPK](/docs/deadlock/basics/pack-a-vpk) and [test it in game](/docs/deadlock/basics/test-your-mod) first. If it uses anyone else's work, read [Credits, licenses, and permissions](/docs/deadlock/publishing/credits-and-licenses) before you start.

Your mod must be a `pakNN_dir.vpk` file. List everyone whose work is in your mod.

## Before you upload

Do these checks before you submit.

1. Test the mod in game with no other mods installed.
2. Check that it gives no gameplay advantage. Camp timers, visibility skins, and louder ability sounds are out. [What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe) has the full list.
3. Check that you have permission for every model, texture, sound, and image you did not make.
4. Leave out AI-made art, models, voices, and music.
5. Keep the mod and its screenshots free of 18+ content.
6. For skins: check that the hero animates and the camera works. Fix any T-pose, missing attachments, or bad distortion.
7. Keep it free. Every mod on Divine is free, so never sell your mod or lock it behind a paywall.

## Build your archive

Upload one archive with only what players need: your VPK and a short readme.

1. Make a new folder for the upload.
2. Copy your `pakNN_dir.vpk` into it.
3. Add a `readme.txt`. Say which pak number to use, which mods it conflicts with, and how to install it by hand.
4. Pack the folder as a `.zip` file. Do not set a password.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/deadlock-publishing-publish-on-divine-1.webp"
  alt="An upload archive holding only pak05_dir.vpk and readme.txt, next to a list of things to leave out: programs, source files, extra game files, and passwords"
/>

A mod never needs a program. Leave out `.exe`, `.dll`, and `.bat` files.

If your mod has more than one VPK, say so in the readme. Tell players how to install every part by hand.

## Take good screenshots

1. Take at least two screenshots in game. Show the front and the back of the hero.
2. Add a close-up of a detail, or an ability your mod changes.
3. Use renders from Blender or other tools only as extras, never instead of in-game shots.
4. For a sound mod, record a short clip that plays your sound.

The showcase guide in Next steps explains how to take clean shots.

## Submit it on Divine

<Steps>
<Step>

### Log in

1. Open [divineskins.gg](https://divineskins.gg).
2. Click **Log in**.
3. No account yet? Click **Create account** instead.

</Step>
<Step>

### Open the Creator Panel

1. Open the [Creator Panel](https://divineskins.gg/creator-panel). This is where you manage your mods.
2. Start a new mod. The new mod form opens.

{/* NEEDS HANDS-ON TEST: the button label in the Creator Panel that opens the new mod form (divineskins.gg/create-draft), and whether a new account needs creator approval before it can upload. */}

</Step>
<Step>

### Fill in the new mod form

1. Write a title that says what the mod is, like "Gold trim for Pocket's briefcase". Skip titles like "My mod".
2. Write a description. Say what the mod changes, which pak number to use, and what it conflicts with.
3. Add your screenshots.
4. Upload your archive.
5. Credit yourself, every co-author, and every creator whose work is in your mod.

The form saves your mod as a **Draft**. You can come back to it later.

{/* NEEDS HANDS-ON TEST: the field labels on the new mod form for a Deadlock mod (game, category, screenshots, files, credits). */}

{/* SCREENSHOT: deadlock-publishing-publish-on-divine-2.webp, the new mod form filled in for an original Divine test mod */}

</Step>
<Step>

### Submit for review

1. Check every field one more time.
2. Click **Submit for review**. Verified creators see **Upload mod** instead.

Your mod moves to **In review**.

{/* NEEDS HANDS-ON TEST: what Upload mod does for verified creators, and whether it skips review. */}

</Step>
<Step>

### Wait for the review

Moderators review every mod before it goes live. Check its state in the Creator Panel.

## Common problems

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="Your mod shows Changes requested">

Read what the moderators asked for. Fix it in the Creator Panel, then submit again.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Your skin T-poses or the camera is wrong">

See [Model and skin problems](/docs/deadlock/errors/model-problems).

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Your mod uses someone else's work">

Ask the creator for permission, save their answer, and credit them. The credits guide linked at the top explains how.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Your archive has a program in it">

Remove it. A mod only needs its VPK and a readme.

</Accordion>
</Accordions>

Still have questions? Hit us up on [Discord](https://discord.gg/divineskins). We're always around.
