GameBanana is where Deadlock mods get published. Upload there once, and players can also install your mod from mod managers like Deadlock Mod Manager. [Pack your mod into a VPK](/docs/deadlock/basics/pack-a-vpk) and [test it in game](/docs/deadlock/basics/test-your-mod) first. If it uses anyone else's work, read [Credits, licenses, and permissions](/docs/deadlock/publishing/credits-and-licenses) before you start.

## What you need

  <Requirement icon="Package">Your finished VPK</Requirement>
  <Requirement icon="GameBanana" href="https://gamebanana.com/games/20948">
    GameBanana account
  </Requirement>
  <Requirement icon="Camera" note="taken in game">
    2+ screenshots
  </Requirement>
  <Requirement icon="FileMusic" note="sound mods only">
    MP3 preview
  </Requirement>
  <Requirement icon="Users">Every creator's name</Requirement>
</Requirements>

Your mod must be a `pakNN_dir.vpk` file. For a GameBanana account, you must be 13 or older, or 16 or older in the EU. The MP3 preview must be 300 KB or less. List everyone whose work is in your mod.

## Before you upload

GameBanana staff hold back or remove mods that fail these checks. Do them first.

1. Read the [Deadlock section rules](https://gamebanana.com/games/rules/20948). They change, so read them again before each upload.
2. Test the mod in game with no other mods installed.
3. Check that it gives no gameplay advantage. Rule R-DL.824 bans things like camp timers, visibility skins, and louder ability sounds. [What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe) has the full list.
4. Check that you have permission for every model, texture, sound, and image you did not make.
5. Check that nothing in the mod or on its page is AI-generated. The Deadlock section bans it.
6. For skins: check that the hero animates and the camera works. Skins that T-pose, lack attachments, or look badly distorted get held back.

## Build your archive

Upload one archive with only what players need: your VPK and a short readme.

1. Make a new folder for the upload.
2. Copy your `pakNN_dir.vpk` into it.
3. Add a `readme.txt`. Say which pak number to use, which mods it conflicts with, and how to install it by hand.
4. Pack the folder as a `.zip`, `.rar`, or `.7z` file. Do not set a password.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/deadlock-publishing-publish-on-gamebanana-2.webp"
  alt="An upload archive holding only pak05_dir.vpk and readme.txt, next to a list of things to leave out: programs, source files, extra game files, and passwords"
/>

A mod never needs a program. If you made a tool, upload it to the Tools section instead. GameBanana's rule R-DL.872 says programs must be open source, or staff must get access to the code.

If your mod has more than one VPK, say so in the readme. Some mod managers break multi-part mods, so tell players how to install it by hand.

## Pick the section and category

GameBanana's Deadlock area has separate sections. Pick the one that matches your mod, then the closest category.

| You made                                        | Section   | Category                         |
| ----------------------------------------------- | --------- | -------------------------------- |
| A hero skin, recolor, or remodel                | Mods      | Skins, then the hero             |
| A soul container, urn, breakable, hideout, NPC  | Mods      | Model Replacement, then the type |
| A HUD restyle or cursor                         | Mods      | HUD, then the type               |
| Portraits, a skybox, or other art               | Mods      | Other/Misc                       |
| An animation                                    | Mods      | Animations                       |
| Ability sounds, voice lines, music, kill sounds | Sounds    | The matching sound category      |
| Unfinished work you want to preview             | WiPs      |                                  |
| A tool for modders                              | Tools     |                                  |
| A written guide                                 | Tutorials |                                  |

Sound mods go in the Sounds section, never in Mods. A GameBanana moderator warned that sound mods posted under Mods get deleted.

Post each mod once, in one section. Posting the same mod twice breaks the site rules.

## Fill in the submission form

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the form

1. Log in to GameBanana.
2. Open the [Deadlock section](https://gamebanana.com/games/20948).
3. Click **Add** in the section menu.
4. Choose the section you picked above.
5. Choose the category.

{/* NEEDS HANDS-ON TEST: the current labels on the Add menu, the section and category picker, and the "Did you create the submission?" question (gap G10, decision D08). */}

{/* SCREENSHOT: deadlock-publishing-publish-on-gamebanana-4.webp, the Add menu in the Deadlock section and the category picker on a test submission */}

</Step>
<Step>

### Title and description

1. Write a title that says what the mod is, like "Gold trim for Pocket's briefcase". Titles like "My mod" are not accepted.
2. Write a description. Say what the mod changes, which pak number to use, and what it conflicts with.
3. Answer "Did you create the submission?" honestly. Choose Yes only if you made it. Converting or re-uploading someone else's mod means No.

</Step>
<Step>

### Screenshots

1. Add at least two screenshots taken in game. Renders from Blender or other tools only count as extras.
2. If your mod has nothing to see, say so in the description.

The showcase guide in Next steps explains how to take clean shots.

</Step>
<Step>

### Credits

1. Open the Credits module on the form.
2. Add yourself and every co-author.
3. Add every creator whose work is in your mod.
4. Add a link for anyone without a GameBanana account.

Credits in the description alone are not enough. GameBanana holds back mods with missing credits. The credits guide linked at the top explains who to credit.

{/* SCREENSHOT: deadlock-publishing-publish-on-gamebanana-5.webp, the Credits module filled in for an original Divine test mod */}

</Step>
<Step>

### License

GameBanana fills in a default license and a permissions checklist. Choose yours on purpose. The credits guide explains the options too.

{/* SCREENSHOT: deadlock-publishing-publish-on-gamebanana-6.webp, the license and permissions checklist on the submission form */}

</Step>
<Step>

### Content rating

Set the content rating if anything in your mod or screenshots is suggestive. GameBanana's Deadlock rules treat Graves and Rem as minors under its indecent content rules. This wiki does not cover 18+ mods.

</Step>
<Step>

### Files

1. Upload your archive.
2. For a sound mod, add the MP3 preview. It must be 300 KB or less and match the sound in the download.
3. Click submit.

Very loud sounds get removed as low quality. A louder or longer version of a quiet ability sound counts as an unfair advantage.

{/* SCREENSHOT: deadlock-publishing-publish-on-gamebanana-7.webp, the file upload panel with an archive attached */}

</Step>
</Steps>

## After you submit

GameBanana scans every file with file analysis and antivirus software. Files that fail are not allowed.

Once your mod is live, its page shows a manual download and 1-Click install buttons for mod managers. Players can install it from Deadlock Mod Manager without touching files.

Sites like deadlockskins.gg copy GameBanana listings and link back to your page. If you do not want that, see [takedowns and aggregator sites](/docs/deadlock/publishing/credits-and-licenses#takedowns-and-aggregator-sites).

## Common problems

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="Your sound mod was deleted">

You posted it under Mods. Post it in the Sounds section.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Your mod is held back for screenshots">

You need two in-game screenshots. Renders alone do not count.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Your skin is held back">

It T-poses, lacks attachments, or looks distorted. See [Model and skin problems](/docs/deadlock/errors/model-problems).

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Your mod is held back for credits">

Fill in the Credits module, not only the description.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Your mod is held back for borrowed work">

Show the staff that the original creator agreed.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Your upload has a program in it">

Remove it, or link its public source code.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="You get a white page when you submit a sound">

This was a GameBanana site bug in October 2026. Try again later.

</Accordion>
</Accordions>
