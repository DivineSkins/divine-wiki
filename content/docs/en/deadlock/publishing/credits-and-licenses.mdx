Every Deadlock mod mixes Valve's game files with your own work, and sometimes with other creators' work. This page covers who owns what, who to credit, which license to pick, and why mods stay free. The upload steps are in [Publish your mod on GameBanana](/docs/deadlock/publishing/publish-on-gamebanana).

## What you need

- A list of every file, image, sound, and model in your mod that you did not make.
- Where each one came from, and who made it.

## Who owns what

<img
  src="/wiki-images/deadlock-publishing-credits-and-licenses-1.webp"
  alt="Three boxes: Valve owns the game's files and they are for non-commercial mods only, you own your own work and pick its license, and other people's work needs their permission first"
/>

- **Valve's files.** Deadlock's models, textures, sounds, and art belong to Valve. The Steam Subscriber Agreement lets you use them for personal, non-commercial purposes. Content made with Valve's tools may only be shared non-commercially. CSDK 12 is built from Valve's CS2 Workshop Tools, so treat your mods the same way.
- **Your work.** Art, meshes, audio, and animation you made yourself are yours. You choose the license.
- **Other people's work.** Other mods, fan art, music, and characters from other games belong to their creators. You need their permission, or a license that allows what you do.

GameBanana has an informal 2015 understanding with Valve. Mods that use Valve's work must be non-profit and credited, and Valve can ask for their removal at any time.

## Credit everyone

1. List every source from your notes in GameBanana's Credits module.
2. Credit the original creator when you recolor or fix their mod.
3. Credit the game or studio for anything from outside Deadlock.
4. Credit voice actors and composers when you use their work.
5. Add a link for anyone who has no GameBanana account.
6. Say plainly which parts you made. For example: "New textures by me. Original model by X."

Put credits in the Credits module, not only in the description. GameBanana holds back mods with missing credits (site rule R.23).

## Pick a license

GameBanana adds a license and a permissions checklist to every mod. The default is Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivs 4.0, or CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. Most popular Deadlock mods keep it.

The letters mean: **BY**, give credit. **NC**, no commercial use. **ND**, no changed versions.

| Can other people...                  | Default answer |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------- |
| Download and install your mod        | Yes            |
| Share it again, on or off GameBanana | Ask first      |
| Change it, or reuse parts of it      | Ask first      |
| Use it in 18+ content                | Ask first      |
| Use it to make money                 | No             |

You can keep the default, change the checklist answers, or write your own terms. Some creators write "ask me first" in their own words. A few popular mods use other licenses, such as CC BY 4.0, GPL-3.0, or Apache-2.0.

Many licenses forbid fixes without permission. If you are fine with others updating your mod after a game update, say so on your mod page.

## Use someone else's mod or art

1. Read the license on the original mod page.
2. Ask the creator before you fix, update, edit, re-upload, or reuse any part.
3. Save their answer. GameBanana staff hold back your mod until you show their consent (rule R-DL.825).
4. Credit them in the Credits module.
5. Say clearly in your description that the original is theirs.

Fan art needs permission too. GameBanana moderators removed most versions of one portrait mod because it used other people's fan art without asking.

Animations, sounds, and music you download from other sites come with their own terms. Read them before you share a mod built on them.

## Characters from other games, films, or anime

A mod that brings a character from another game, film, or anime into Deadlock is a port. GameBanana handles ports through its [Porting Whitelist and Guidelines](https://gamebanana.com/wikis/755):

- Studios sit on a Confirmed, Grey Area, or Definite No list.
- Ports from Definite No studios are not allowed.
- Any port can be removed after a copyright (DMCA) takedown.

If you make a port, credit the original studio, plus any voice actors and composers. This wiki teaches with original art only. It does not feature or link ports.

## AI-made content

GameBanana's Deadlock section bans AI-generated content in mods and on mod pages (rule R-DL.847). That covers renders, textures, models, voices, and music.

AI-assisted code is the one exception. You must say so at the top of your mod page, in bold, underlined, readable text.

r/DeadlockTheGame bans AI posts too.

## Keep it free

**Never sell a Deadlock mod.** Valve's Steam Subscriber Agreement allows only non-commercial sharing of content made with its tools. GameBanana forbids selling mods.

- GameBanana allows one kind of paywall: early access, for 30 days at most. After that, the mod must be free for everyone.
- A fix for a new game version can never sit behind early access.
- If a mod breaks these rules, GameBanana's fix is a free download on the mod page.

The one paid Deadlock skin, in April 2026, drew a strong backlash from players. The community expects mods to be free.

## Takedowns and aggregator sites

Some sites, like deadlockskins.gg, copy GameBanana listings automatically and link back to GameBanana.

- **To leave deadlockskins.gg:** use the Report button on your mod there, or email the site. It says it honors removal requests from creators.
- **For other sites:** send a copyright (DMCA) notice to the site or to its host. The GameBanana tutorial [Submitting a Takedown to a Website](https://gamebanana.com/tuts/20265) walks through it.
- **If someone files a takedown against your mod:** GameBanana removes mods after a verified takedown. You can appeal through a support ticket.
