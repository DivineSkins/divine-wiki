Your mod works in your game. These guides help you share it the right way, credit the people behind it, and keep it working after updates. Every Deadlock mod stays free: never sell one.

## What do you want to do?

### Publish your mod

- **Goal:** Upload your mod so players can find and install it.
- **Method:** Pack a clean archive, pick the right GameBanana section, and fill in screenshots, credits, and a license.
- **Guide:** [Publish your mod on GameBanana](/docs/deadlock/publishing/publish-on-gamebanana)

---

### Credit others and pick a license

- **Goal:** Know what you can reuse, who to credit, and which license to choose.
- **Method:** Ask before you reuse other work, fill in the Credits module, and choose a license on purpose.
- **Guide:** [Credits, licenses, and permissions](/docs/deadlock/publishing/credits-and-licenses)

---

### Fix your mod after a game update

- **Goal:** Get a broken mod working again on the new game build.
- **Method:** Find what Valve changed, rebuild on the new files, and post the fix on the same mod page.
- **Guide:** [Update your mod after a game update](/docs/deadlock/publishing/update-after-a-patch)

---

### Show off your mod

- **Goal:** Get clean screenshots and a short clip, and share them on Reddit and Discord.
- **Method:** Shoot in a private match, then post with full credits.
- **Guide:** [Show off your mod](/docs/deadlock/publishing/showcase)
