Your mod works in your game. These guides help you share it the right way, credit the people behind it, and keep it working after updates. Every Deadlock mod stays free: never sell one.

## What do you want to do?

<ToolCard
  name="Publish your mod on GameBanana"
  href="/docs/deadlock/publishing/publish-on-gamebanana"
  badge="Start here"
>
  Upload your mod so players can find and install it. Pack a clean archive, pick
  the right section, and fill in screenshots, credits, and a license.
</ToolCard>
<ToolCard
  name="Credits, licenses, and permissions"
  href="/docs/deadlock/publishing/credits-and-licenses"
>
  Know what you can reuse, who to credit, and which license to choose. Ask
  first, fill in the Credits module, and pick a license on purpose.
</ToolCard>
<ToolCard
  name="Update your mod after a game update"
  href="/docs/deadlock/publishing/update-after-a-patch"
>
  Get a broken mod working again. Find what Valve changed, rebuild on the new
  files, and post the fix on the same mod page.
</ToolCard>
<ToolCard name="Show off your mod" href="/docs/deadlock/publishing/showcase">
  Get clean screenshots and a short clip in a private match, then share them on
  Reddit and Discord with full credits.
</ToolCard>
