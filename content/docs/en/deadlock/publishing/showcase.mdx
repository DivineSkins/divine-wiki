You made a mod. Now show it. This page covers screenshots, a short clip, and where to post them. Publish your mod first, so people have somewhere to download it: [Publish your mod on GameBanana](/docs/deadlock/publishing/publish-on-gamebanana).

## What you need

- Your finished mod, tested with [Test your mod in game](/docs/deadlock/basics/test-your-mod).
- A screenshot tool and a screen recorder. The ones you already use are fine.
- A GameBanana page for your mod, so you have a link to share.

## Keep it free and fair

- **Keep it free.** Never sell your mod or lock it behind a paywall. Valve's Steam Subscriber Agreement allows only non-commercial use of content made with its tools.
- **Show only cosmetic mods.** Never show off a mod that makes enemies easier to see or hear. [What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe) lists the rules.
- **Show your own work.** Use art you made, or art you have permission to use. [Credits, licenses, and permissions](/docs/deadlock/publishing/credits-and-licenses) explains why characters from other games or films are a risk.
- **No AI-made art, models, voices, or music.** GameBanana's Deadlock section and r/DeadlockTheGame both ban them.
- **Keep it free of 18+ content.** Your screenshots and clips should be fine for anyone to see.

## Take clean screenshots

<img
  src="/wiki-images/deadlock-publishing-showcase-2.webp"
  alt="A good screenshot set: the hero from the front, from the back, a close-up of a detail, and an ability that changes the model"
/>

1. Turn off your other mods. Only your mod should show.
2. Start Deadlock and load a private map, such as `map dl_hideout`. [Test your mod in game](/docs/deadlock/basics/test-your-mod) shows how to use the console.
3. Try the sandbox mode for better lighting. Some creators test their skins there.
4. Pick your hero. Type `changeteam 0` in the console to pick again.
5. Take shots from the front and the back. Add a close-up and one ability that changes the model.
6. Take at least two in-game screenshots. GameBanana needs them for every mod page.
7. Use Steam's screenshot key, F12 by default, or your own tool.

Show the mod as it looks in game. A render from Blender can come as an extra, never instead of real screenshots.

## Record a short clip

A clip shows what screenshots cannot: animations, effects, and sound.

1. Use the same private map, with only your mod on.
2. Start your screen recorder.
3. Show the mod moving. Walk, turn, and use the abilities your mod changes.
4. For a sound mod, record the game audio. Play your sound a few times.
5. Keep the clip short. Cut anything that does not show your mod.

Sound mods have no screenshots, so a clip is your best showcase. GameBanana's Sounds section also asks for a short MP3 preview.

Skip camera tools that hook into the running game or read its memory. You do not need them to show off a mod.

## Post on Reddit

[r/DeadlockTheGame](https://www.reddit.com/r/DeadlockTheGame/) is where mod showcases go. There is no separate subreddit for Deadlock modding. Good showcases there often get thousands of upvotes.

1. Read the subreddit's rules before you post.
2. Write a clear title with the mod's name and the hero.
3. Add your screenshots or your clip.
4. Credit everyone whose work is in your mod.
5. Add the link to your GameBanana page.

The rules that matter most for mod posts, as of October 2026:

| Rule   | What it says                              |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| Rule 3 | Credit the creators of the work you post. |
| Rule 4 | No AI-generated content.                  |
| Rule 7 | Follow the rules for NSFW content.        |
| Rule 8 | Follow Valve's terms. No unfair play.     |

## Share on Discord

- **[Deadlock Modding](https://discord.gg/P2PCCtYt2B)** is the main server for creators. Ask for feedback and share your work there. To join, you reportedly need access to Valve's official Deadlock Discord, which is invite-only.
- **[Deadlock GB Community](https://discord.gg/CaNPxMQeFN)** is a public server run by GameBanana's Deadlock section managers. Ask there for feedback on a GameBanana submission.

Read each server's rules and post in the channel meant for sharing work.

Want feedback before you release? Post a preview in the WiPs section on GameBanana. It must be your own work. Some creators also post previews on X.

## Credit everyone

1. Name everyone whose work is in your mod. Include model and texture makers, co-creators, voice actors, composers, and sound sources.
2. Link to their pages, so people can find their work.
3. Ask first before you fix, recolor, or build on another creator's mod. Many licenses forbid changes without permission. GameBanana holds back mods until the author agrees.
4. Use the same credits in every post: GameBanana, Reddit, and Discord.

Thanking the guides and tools you used is a nice touch too.
