Particle mods change how ability effects look in your own game. Keep them as readable as the originals: never brighter, bigger, fainter, or removed. Read [What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe) before you start.

## What do you want to do?

### Recolor an ability's effects

- **Goal:** Give an ability's effects new colors, for example to match your skin.
- **Method:** Find the particles with `dumpparticlelist`, change their colors in CSDK 12's Particle Editor or vpcf-editor, then compile and pack them.
- **Guide:** [Recolor ability effects](/docs/deadlock/particles/recolor-ability-effects)

---

### Make a new effect

- **Goal:** Build your own effect and use it in place of an existing one.
- **Method:** Build it in the Particle Editor, then save it at the original effect's path. Learn recolors first.
- **Guide:** [Make a new particle effect](/docs/deadlock/particles/make-an-effect)
