Particle mods change how ability effects look in your own game. Keep them as readable as the originals: never brighter, bigger, fainter, or removed. Read [What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe) before you start.

## What do you want to do?

<ToolCard
  name="Recolor ability effects"
  href="/docs/deadlock/particles/recolor-ability-effects"
  badge="Start here"
>
  Give an ability's effects new colors, for example to match your skin. Find the
  particles with `dumpparticlelist`, recolor them in the Particle Editor or
  vpcf-editor, then compile and pack.
</ToolCard>
<ToolCard
  name="Make a new particle effect"
  href="/docs/deadlock/particles/make-an-effect"
  badge="Advanced"
>
  Build your own effect in the Particle Editor and play it in place of an
  existing one. Learn recolors first.
</ToolCard>
