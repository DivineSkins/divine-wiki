UI mods change how the game looks, not what it tells you. Never add timers, health numbers, or brighter objective icons. Read [What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe) before you start.

## What do you want to do?

### Replace portraits, icons, or item art

- **Goal:** Swap hero portraits, minimap icons, ability icons, or shop item art for your own art.
- **Method:** Compile your images with CSDK 12's image template, rename them, and pack them. Or use Texture Forge in your browser.
- **Guide:** [Replace portraits, icons, and item art](/docs/deadlock/ui/portraits-and-icons)
