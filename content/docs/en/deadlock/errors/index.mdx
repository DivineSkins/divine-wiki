Pick the problem you see. Each guide puts the exact error text first, so you can match it to your screen. After a game update, start with the first two.

## What is going wrong?

### Mods do nothing in game

- **Pick this if:** the game starts fine, but your skins, sounds, or icons do not show.
- **Guide:** [Mods not loading](/docs/deadlock/errors/mods-not-loading)

---

### The game crashes or will not queue

- **Pick this if:** you see a `FATAL ERROR`, the game closes at launch, or matchmaking refuses to start.
- **Guide:** [Crashes, launch errors, and matchmaking blocks](/docs/deadlock/errors/game-crashes)

---

### Your own mod does not work

- **Pick this if:** CSDK 12 will not start, a compile fails, or your change never shows in game.
- **Guide:** [Errors when making mods](/docs/deadlock/errors/creator-errors)

---

### A skin looks wrong

- **Pick this if:** a hero T-poses, the camera sits in the middle of the screen, or the health bar is at the feet.
- **Guide:** [Model and skin problems](/docs/deadlock/errors/model-problems)

---

### Deadlock Mod Manager acts up

- **Pick this if:** the app will not start, antivirus flags it, downloads get stuck, or it cannot find the game.
- **Guide:** [Deadlock Mod Manager problems](/docs/deadlock/errors/mod-manager-problems)

---

### You play on Linux or Steam Deck

- **Pick this if:** you cannot find the game folder, or the mod manager window stays grey.
- **Guide:** [Linux and Steam Deck](/docs/deadlock/errors/linux-steam-deck)
