Sound mods change what you hear in your own game. Keep them fair: never make enemy cues louder or longer. Read [What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe) before you start.

## What do you want to do?

### Replace one sound

- **Goal:** Swap an ability sound or a music track for your own audio.
- **Method:** Find the sound in Source 2 Viewer, compile your file with CSDK 12, and pack it. Or use Sound Forge in your browser.
- **Guide:** [Replace a sound](/docs/deadlock/sounds/replace-a-sound)

---
