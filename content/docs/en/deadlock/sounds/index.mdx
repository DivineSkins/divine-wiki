Sound mods change what you hear in your own game. Keep them fair: never make enemy cues louder or longer. Read [What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe) before you start.

## What do you want to do?

- [Replace a sound](/docs/deadlock/sounds/replace-a-sound): swap an ability sound or music track for your own audio, with CSDK 12 or Sound Forge in your browser.
- [Replace voice lines](/docs/deadlock/sounds/voice-lines): change one line or a hero's whole voice, through the game's talker files.
- [Play random sounds with soundevents](/docs/deadlock/sounds/random-sounds): make one ability pick a different sound each time.
- [Loop and compress sounds](/docs/deadlock/sounds/looping-and-compression): make a looping ability sound play smoothly, or shrink a large sound mod.
