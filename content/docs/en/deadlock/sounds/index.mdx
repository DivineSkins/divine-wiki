---
title: "Sound modding"
description: "Guides for custom Deadlock sounds: replace ability sounds and music, swap voice lines, and play random sounds, without louder enemy cues."
category: "sounds"
patch: "6779"
---

Sound mods change what you hear in your own game. Keep them fair: never make enemy cues louder or longer. Read [What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe) before you start.

## What do you want to do?

<ToolCard
  name="Replace a sound"
  href="/docs/deadlock/sounds/replace-a-sound"
  badge="Beginner"
>
  Swap an ability sound or music track for your own audio, with CSDK 12 or Sound
  Forge in your browser.
</ToolCard>
<ToolCard name="Replace voice lines" href="/docs/deadlock/sounds/voice-lines">
  Change one line or a hero's whole voice, through the game's talker files.
</ToolCard>
<ToolCard
  name="Play random sounds with soundevents"
  href="/docs/deadlock/sounds/random-sounds"
>
  Make one ability pick a different sound each time.
</ToolCard>
<ToolCard
  name="Loop and compress sounds"
  href="/docs/deadlock/sounds/looping-and-compression"
  badge="Untested"
>
  Make a looping ability sound play smoothly, or shrink a large sound mod.
</ToolCard>
