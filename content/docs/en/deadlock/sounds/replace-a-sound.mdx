<LevelPill level="beginner" />

Replace one ability sound or music track with your own audio: find it in Source 2 Viewer, compile your file with CSDK 12, and pack it as a mod. New to mods? Read [How Deadlock mods work](/docs/deadlock/basics/how-mods-work) and [Set up your creator tools](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/creator-setup) first.

## What you need

<ToolCard name="Source 2 Viewer" href="/docs/deadlock/tools/source-2-viewer">
  Finds the original sound and exports it.
</ToolCard>
<ToolCard name="CSDK 12" href="/docs/deadlock/tools/csdk-12">
  Compiles your audio into a sound the game reads.
</ToolCard>

- An audio editor, such as Audacity (free).
- Your own audio. Use sounds you made or have permission to share.
- The hero's codename.

## What you can change

Other players hear the normal game. You hear your sound on every copy of that hero, enemies included.

- Change your hero's ability sounds, music, or menu sounds.
- Keep the new sound about as loud and as long as the original.
- For your first mod, pick a sound that plays once. Sounds that loop while an ability is active need extra steps.

<Callout type="danger" title="Never make warning cues easier to hear">
  Do not make enemy cues louder, longer, or clearer. This includes heavy melee,
  parry, ultimates, and footsteps. Players see these mods as unfair. Read
  [What's allowed and how to stay
  safe](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe).
</Callout>

## Make the mod

Pick a route. The full workflow gives you the most control. The quick option builds the mod in your browser, with no CSDK 12.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Find the sound

1. Open Source 2 Viewer.
2. Open `Deadlock/game/citadel/pak01_dir.vpk`.
3. Go to `sounds/abilities/` and open your hero's folder. Folders use codenames, not hero names. Look yours up in [Hero names and file paths](/docs/deadlock/basics/hero-codenames). Music is in `sounds/music/`.
4. Double-click sounds to play them until you find the one you want.
5. Write down the full path and file name exactly, letter by letter.
6. Right-click the sound and choose **Decompile & export**. Save it outside your addon, to compare length and volume later.

{/* NEEDS HANDS-ON TEST: the City Never Sleeps update (2026-09-29) changed many sounds. Pick the example sound for this page (a one-shot ability sound) and confirm its path on build 6779. */}

You can also find a sound in game. Load a private map, open the console, and type `snd_showstart 1`. Then use the ability. The console prints the path of each sound as it starts.

{/* NEEDS HANDS-ON TEST: whether snd_showstart 1 works without the -dev launch option or sv_cheats. */}

</Step>
<Step>

### Start from the Billy Audio Template

CSDK 12 comes with a ready-made sound addon. Copy it instead of building folders by hand.

1. Open the CSDK 12 launcher. Keep Steam running.
2. Right-click **Billy Audio Template** and choose **Duplicate Addon**.
3. Name the copy in lowercase with no spaces, for example `mysound`. The launcher hides names with capitals, spaces, or special characters.
4. Right-click your new addon and choose **Explore Content Folder**. You see a `sounds` folder and a `soundevents` folder.
5. Delete the Billy sound files inside `sounds`. If you keep them, your mod also changes one of Billy's sounds.

Leave the `soundevents` folder alone and do not pack it.

{/* SCREENSHOT: deadlock-sounds-replace-a-sound-4.webp, the CSDK 12 launcher with Duplicate Addon on Billy Audio Template */}

{/* NEEDS HANDS-ON TEST: launcher menu labels (Duplicate Addon, Explore Content Folder) and the template's exact contents in the current CSDK 12 zip. */}

</Step>
<Step>

### Add your audio

1. Inside the addon's `sounds` folder, rebuild the original folder path, such as `abilities/<codename>/`.
2. Open the original sound and your new audio in Audacity.
3. Trim your audio to about the same length as the original.
4. Match the volume. If yours is too quiet, use **Effect > Volume and Compression > Amplify**.
5. Export it as `.wav` or `.mp3`.
6. Name it exactly like the original, in lowercase. Only the ending changes: `example_sound.vsnd_c` in the game becomes `example_sound.wav` in your addon.
7. Save it in the folder you made in step 1.

Your addon's content folder now looks like this:

<Step>

### Compile

1. In the CSDK 12 launcher, select your addon and click **Launch Tools**. The Asset Browser opens.
2. Find your new sound in the Asset Browser.
3. Right-click it and choose **Recompile > Full**.
4. Right-click your addon in the launcher and choose **Explore Game Folder**.
5. Open the same path, such as `sounds/abilities/<codename>/`. Your compiled `.vsnd_c` file is there.

{/* SCREENSHOT: deadlock-sounds-replace-a-sound-6.webp, Asset Browser right-click menu with Recompile > Full on the sound */}

{/* SCREENSHOT: deadlock-sounds-replace-a-sound-7.webp, the addon game folder with the compiled .vsnd_c */}

</Step>
<Step>

### Pack and test

Take the compiled file from the game folder. The content folder only holds your source audio.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/deadlock-sounds-replace-a-sound-8.webp"
  alt="Your .wav in the content folder compiles to a .vsnd_c in the game folder. You copy it into a staging folder at the same path and pack it as pak05_dir.vpk."
/>

1. Make a staging folder, such as `mysound_pack`, with the same path inside: `sounds/abilities/<codename>/`.
2. Copy only your compiled `.vsnd_c` file into it.
3. Pack the folder into a VPK with [Pack your mod into a VPK](/docs/deadlock/basics/pack-a-vpk).
4. Load the mod and check the sound with [Test your mod in game](/docs/deadlock/basics/test-your-mod).

</Step>
</Steps>

<Tab value="Quick option in the browser">

Sound Forge on Deadlock Forge builds a sound mod in your browser. Other browser tools are in [Make mods without installing tools](/docs/deadlock/tools/browser-builders).

1. Open [Sound Forge](https://deadlockforge.net/sound/).
2. Filter the list by category or hero, or search by file name or voice line text. Then click the sound you want to replace.
3. Drop in your audio file. It accepts mp3, wav, ogg, and flac.
4. Trim the sound and set its volume. Keep it close to the original.
5. Click **Forge VPK** and download the file.
6. Rename the file to a name like `pak05_dir.vpk`. The naming rules are in [Pack your mod into a VPK](/docs/deadlock/basics/pack-a-vpk).
7. Install it with [Deadlock Mod Manager](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/install-with-mod-manager) or [by hand](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/install-by-hand).

## Common problems

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="The game plays the old sound">

Your path or file name differs from the original. Check every letter in Source 2 Viewer.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Nothing changes at all">

Copy the `.vsnd_c` from the game folder, not the content folder.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Another mod wins">

Give your mod a lower pak number, or remove the other mod.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The sound is cut off">

The game decides how long a sound plays. Trim your audio to the original length.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The sound is too quiet">

Raise it with Amplify in Audacity, but not above the original.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The sound plays at a higher pitch">

One creator reports that some hero sounds are pitched up when they play. This may differ by hero. Open the hero's file in `soundevents/hero/` in Source 2 Viewer and look for a pitch value on that sound.

{/* NEEDS HANDS-ON TEST: the "pitched up 25%" claim (conflict C48). Check the pitch value in a decompiled hero .vsndevts before telling readers to pitch their audio down. */}

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="It worked before an update">

Updates can move or change sounds. Find the sound again and check the path.

</Accordion>
</Accordions>

More fixes are in [Errors when making mods](/docs/deadlock/errors/creator-errors).
