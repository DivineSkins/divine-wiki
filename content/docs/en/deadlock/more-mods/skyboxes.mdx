Source 2 Viewer finds the sky files and decompiles them. CSDK 12 compiles your new sky. Use sky images you made or have permission to use.

## What a sky mod may change

A sky mod swaps the picture above the map. Mods are client-side, so only you see the new sky.

Keep the light the same. Brighter or darker lighting, less fog, bloom, and extra contrast all change how easy enemies are to see. [What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe) lists these as mods we never cover.

<img
  src="/wiki-images/deadlock-more-mods-skyboxes-1.webp"
  alt="Two boxes: swapping the sky art is fine; changing lighting, fog, bloom, filters, or contrast is off limits"
/>

Some GameBanana "map" mods for Deadlock are really lighting or night mods for the main map. This guide does not cover them.

## Make the sky mod

<Steps>
<Step>

### Find the sky files

Our notes do not name the sky files on the current build. The fastest way to find them is to look inside a sky mod that already exists.

1. Download a sky mod from GameBanana, for example [Skybox Replacement](https://gamebanana.com/mods/600744) by HyperLine. It has 31 skies made from HDRI photos. Use it to learn the paths only. Do not reuse its art.
2. Open one of its VPKs in Source 2 Viewer.
3. Write down every file path inside. These are the files a sky mod replaces.
4. Open `Deadlock/game/citadel/pak01_dir.vpk` in Source 2 Viewer and look for the same paths.
5. If they are not there, open the main map file, `game/citadel/maps/dl_midtown.vpk`, and look there.

That mod was last updated in September 2025, before several big game updates. Check that each path still exists today.

### Replace the images

These are the normal texture steps. We have not tested them on a sky yet.

1. Create a CSDK 12 addon. Use a lowercase name with no spaces, for example `mysky`.
2. Decompile the sky file into `content/citadel_addons/mysky/`, at the same path as in the game.
3. Open the sky image in your image editor. Note its size and layout.
4. Put your art in its place. Keep the same size and layout, and keep it about as bright as the original.
5. Save it with the same name, over the decompiled image.
6. Click **Launch Tools** for your addon. In the Asset Browser, right-click the sky file and choose <MenuPath path="Recompile > Full" />.
7. Find the compiled `_c` files in `game/citadel_addons/mysky/`.

If the sky is a material, ship the new `.vmat_c` and the new `.vtex_c` files it points to. A compiled material names its textures with a hash, so the old texture name will not work.

{/* NEEDS HANDS-ON TEST: the whole section on build 6779. Check the sky image size and layout (one wide image or several faces), whether Recompile > Full works on it, and whether the sky texture also feeds the map's lighting or reflections. */}

</Step>
<Step>

### Pack and test

1. Copy only the compiled files you changed into a staging folder, with the same paths.
2. Pack the folder into a VPK. Follow [Pack your mod into a VPK](/docs/deadlock/basics/pack-a-vpk).
3. Load the main map on your own PC with `map dl_midtown`. [Test your mod in game](/docs/deadlock/basics/test-your-mod) shows how.
4. Look up and check the new sky.
5. Compare with the normal game. Heroes, walls, and the ground should look as bright as before.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Common problems

- **The sky does not change.** Your path or file name is different from the original. Check every letter against Source 2 Viewer.
- **The map looks brighter or darker.** Remove every file that is not sky art. If it still looks different, do not share the mod.
- **It worked before an update.** Game updates can move or change files. Find the sky files again and check the paths.
