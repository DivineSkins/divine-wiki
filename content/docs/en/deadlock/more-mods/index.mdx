Each mod here changes only your own game. Read [What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe) before you start.

## What do you want to do?

<CardGrid>
  <PremiumCard
    title="Replace the skybox"
    href="/docs/deadlock/more-mods/skyboxes"
    icon={<Icon name="Cloud" />}
    level="intermediate"
  >
    Untested. Swap the sky images above the map for your own art and pack them
    as a VPK. Lighting and fog stay as they are.
  </PremiumCard>
  <PremiumCard
    title="Custom mouse cursors"
    href="/docs/deadlock/more-mods/cursors"
    icon={<Icon name="MousePointer2" />}
    level="beginner"
  >
    Replace the cursor files in the game folder with your own image. No modding
    tools needed.
  </PremiumCard>
  <PremiumCard
    title="Custom chat wheel"
    href="/docs/deadlock/more-mods/chat-wheel"
    icon={<Icon name="MessageSquare" />}
    level="intermediate"
  >
    Untested. Add custom chat wheel menus and extra voice commands. Build them
    in ChatLane, a free community tool, and install the VPK it makes.
  </PremiumCard>
  <PremiumCard
    title="Play custom maps"
    href="/docs/deadlock/more-mods/custom-maps"
    icon={<Icon name="Map" />}
    level="beginner"
  >
    Try a community map, like a movement training map, on your own. Put it in
    the `maps` folder and load it from the console.
  </PremiumCard>
</CardGrid>
