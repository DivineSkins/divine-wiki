Each mod here changes only your own game. Read [What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe) before you start.

## What do you want to do?

<ToolCard
  name="Replace the skybox"
  href="/docs/deadlock/more-mods/skyboxes"
  badge="Untested"
>
  Swap the sky images above the map for your own art and pack them as a VPK.
  Lighting and fog stay as they are.
</ToolCard>
<ToolCard name="Custom mouse cursors" href="/docs/deadlock/more-mods/cursors">
  Replace the cursor files in the game folder with your own image. No modding
  tools needed.
</ToolCard>
<ToolCard
  name="Custom chat wheel"
  href="/docs/deadlock/more-mods/chat-wheel"
  badge="Untested"
>
  Add custom chat wheel menus and extra voice commands. Build them in ChatLane,
  a free community tool, and install the VPK it makes.
</ToolCard>
<ToolCard name="Play custom maps" href="/docs/deadlock/more-mods/custom-maps">
  Try a community map, like a movement training map, on your own. Put it in the
  `maps` folder and load it from the console.
</ToolCard>
