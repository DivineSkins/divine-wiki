Small cosmetic mods that don't fit the other sections. Each one changes only your own game. Read [What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe) before you start.

## What do you want to do?

### Change the sky

- **Goal:** Replace the sky above the map with your own art.
- **Method:** Swap the sky images and pack them as a VPK. Lighting and fog stay as they are. Not tested on the current build yet.
- **Guide:** [Replace the skybox](/docs/deadlock/more-mods/skyboxes)

---

### Change your mouse cursor

- **Goal:** Use your own image as the mouse cursor.
- **Method:** Replace the cursor files in the game folder. No modding tools needed.
- **Guide:** [Custom mouse cursors](/docs/deadlock/more-mods/cursors)

---

### Build your own chat wheel

- **Goal:** Add custom chat wheel menus and extra voice commands.
- **Method:** Build the wheel in ChatLane, a free community tool, and install the VPK it makes.
- **Guide:** [Custom chat wheel](/docs/deadlock/more-mods/chat-wheel)

---

### Play a custom map

- **Goal:** Try a community map, like a movement training map, on your own.
- **Method:** Put the map in the `maps` folder and load it from the console.
- **Guide:** [Play custom maps](/docs/deadlock/more-mods/custom-maps)
