Animation mods replace one of a hero's existing clips with your own motion. You cannot add new moves: that needs the game's animation graph, and no public tool can edit it. These guides are advanced, so get comfortable with Blender first.

Keep your animations fair. Never make an enemy's wind-up easier to read, and never shrink a hero on screen. Read [What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe) before you start.
