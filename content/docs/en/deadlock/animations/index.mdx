Animation mods replace one of a hero's existing clips with your own motion. You cannot add new moves: that needs the game's animation graph, and no public tool can edit it. These guides are advanced. Get comfortable with Blender first: start with [Import a Deadlock model into Blender](/docs/deadlock/models/import-into-blender).

Keep your animations fair. Never make an enemy's wind-up easier to read, and never shrink a hero on screen. Read [What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe) before you start.

## What do you want to do?

### Replace one animation

- **Goal:** Swap an ability, melee, parry, or reload animation for your own motion.
- **Method:** Animate in Blender, export a DMX, and point the game's clip file at it. Pack only the compiled clip.
- **Guide:** [Replace an animation](/docs/deadlock/animations/replace-an-animation)

---

### Build clips in ModelDoc

- **Goal:** Get full control over clips, events, aim clips, and IK chains.
- **Method:** Import your DMX animations in ModelDoc and generate the clip files there.
- **Guide:** [Animation clips in ModelDoc](/docs/deadlock/animations/ik-and-modeldoc)
