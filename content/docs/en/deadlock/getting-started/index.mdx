New to Deadlock modding? This page shows what a mod is, what you can make, and which guide to read first. You do not need any tools yet.

## What a Deadlock mod is

A Deadlock mod is a `.vpk` file. It holds new copies of game files, such as a hero texture or a sound, at the same paths as the files they replace.

The game loads mods from one folder: `game/citadel/addons/`. A mod manager sets this up for you, or you can do it by hand.

Mods are client-side. They change the game on your PC only. [How Deadlock mods work](/docs/deadlock/basics/how-mods-work) has the details.

## What you can make

This wiki covers mods that change how the game looks and sounds:

- Hero skins, recolors, and materials.
- Sounds for your own hero, voice lines, and music.
- Hero portraits, icons, item art, and menu art.
- Text, such as item names.

We never cover mods that give you an advantage: timers, enemy info, louder enemy sounds, bright or see-through skins, smaller models. [What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe) lists them all.

## What you need

To play with mods:

- Deadlock on Steam, updated to the latest version.
- A Windows or Linux PC.
- [Deadlock Mod Manager](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/install-with-mod-manager), or a text editor to [install mods by hand](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/install-by-hand).

To make your own mods:

- A Windows PC. The main creator tools run on Windows only.
- [Source 2 Viewer](/docs/deadlock/tools/source-2-viewer), to open and export game files.
- [CSDK 12](/docs/deadlock/tools/csdk-12), a free, unofficial community toolset, to compile your files.
- An image editor, such as GIMP or Photoshop, and an audio editor, such as Audacity.
- [Blender](/docs/deadlock/tools/blender-add-ons), later, if you want to change 3D models.

[Set up your creator tools](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/creator-setup) walks you through each install.

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordions type="multiple">

<Accordion title="Is modding Deadlock allowed?">

Valve has published no mod policy for Deadlock, so nobody can promise what is allowed. The game is still an invite-only playtest, and Valve lists mod support as "Unsupported". The choice to use mods is yours.

Read [What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe) before you install anything.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do other players see my mods?">

No. You see a skin on every copy of that hero in your game, enemies included. Nobody else sees it.

This is why a bright skin can give you an unfair advantage by accident.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there an official modding kit?">

No. Valve ships no modding tools for Deadlock. The community built its own toolset, CSDK 12, from Valve's Counter-Strike 2 Workshop Tools.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do mods break after a game update?">

Often, yes. Updates replace the file that tells the game to load mods. A mod manager redoes the edit the next time you launch through it. [Install mods by hand](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/install-by-hand#after-a-game-update) shows the manual fix.

Big updates also break some mods, most of all HUD mods. Those stay broken until their creators [update them](/docs/deadlock/publishing/update-after-a-patch).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I sell my mods?">

No. Valve's Steam Subscriber Agreement allows only non-commercial use of content made with its tools. GameBanana does not allow selling mods either.

[Credits, licenses, and permissions](/docs/deadlock/publishing/credits-and-licenses) explains what you can share.

</Accordion>

</Accordions>

## Where to get help

- **GameBanana:** the main place to download Deadlock mods and ask questions. Open the [Deadlock section](https://gamebanana.com/games/20948).
- **Deadlock Modding Discord:** most creators help each other here. It may ask you to verify through Valve's official Deadlock Discord first. [Join the server](https://discord.gg/P2PCCtYt2B).
- **Deadlock GB Community Discord:** a public server run by GameBanana's Deadlock team. Use it if you cannot get into the modding Discord. [Join the server](https://discord.gg/CaNPxMQeFN).
- **Deadlock Modding Notes:** a community site with written guides. [Open the site](https://deadlockmodding.pages.dev/).
