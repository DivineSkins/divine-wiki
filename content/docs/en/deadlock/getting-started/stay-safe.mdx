Read this page before you install or make your first mod. It covers what mods can change, which mods we never cover, and how to avoid malware. The install steps live in [Install mods with Deadlock Mod Manager](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/install-with-mod-manager) and [Install mods by hand](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/install-by-hand).

## What you need

- Nothing to install.
- An archive tool, such as 7-Zip, to look inside mod downloads.

## How mods work in a match

Mods are client-side. They change files on your PC only.

You see a skin on every player who uses that hero, enemies included. Nobody else sees your mods. Other players see the normal game.

This means a skin can give you an advantage by accident. A bright skin on an enemy hero makes that enemy easier for you to spot.

## What Valve has said

Valve has published no mod policy for Deadlock. Its patch notes and announcements never mention player mods. The game is still an invite-only playtest, and Valve lists mod support as "Unsupported".

Valve has made changes inside the game, though:

- Since March 2026, the game can refuse to start matchmaking. This happens when anyone in your party runs the creator tools, or has changed certain parts of `gameinfo.gi`.
- Since September 2026, the game ignores many visibility settings in `gameinfo.gi`, such as enemy outlines and glow.

As of October 2026, no one has reported a confirmed ban for a cosmetic mod. That is not a promise, and Valve can change its rules at any time. The Steam Subscriber Agreement lets Valve close accounts that use mods for an unfair advantage.

A ban can also hit your friends. Reports from 2024 say that a ban for unfair play also banned every player that account invited to the playtest.

## Mods you can make

This wiki covers mods that change how your game looks and sounds:

- Hero skins, recolors, and materials.
- Sounds for your own hero, voice lines, and music.
- Hero portraits, icons, item art, and menu art.
- Text, such as item names.
- Packing, installing, and publishing your mod.

Before you share a mod, ask one question: does it show me anything the normal game hides? If the answer is yes, it crosses the line.

Check your skin from the enemy's point of view too. A skin that glows or turns very dark can make that hero easier to spot.

## Mods we never cover

These mods give you an advantage over players without them. GameBanana's Deadlock rules ban many of them, and players see them as unfair. This wiki will never explain, link, or show them.

| Type                   | Examples                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Timers and trackers    | Camp or buff respawn timers, cooldown or soul trackers, enemy health numbers, ranks, missing-enemy alerts, announcer packs that call objectives                                                 |
| Louder enemy cues      | Louder or longer heavy melee, parry, ultimate, or footstep sounds                                                                                                                               |
| Visibility changes     | Glowing, bright, see-through, or very dark skins; skins you can see through veils or fog; removed outlines, particles, or smoke; brighter lighting; fog turned off; a wider view; scope removal |
| Size and game data     | Smaller, thinner, or taller hero models; hitbox changes; camera changes; gameplay data changes                                                                                                  |
| Config and code tricks | Console variable edits in `gameinfo.gi`, code injection, overlays, "mod menus"                                                                                                                  |

Model size matters in Deadlock. A smaller or thinner hero blocks less of your own screen. A taller one can let you see over cover. Keep every skin the same size as the original.

## Keep your account in good standing

1. Edit only the `SearchPaths` block of `gameinfo.gi`. It is the only part a mod needs.
2. Leave every other section of that file alone. Changes to `Engine2`, `MaterialSystem2`, `NetworkSystem`, `Particles`, `RenderSystem`, `SceneSystem`, or `WorldRenderer` can block matchmaking for your whole party.
3. Never paste a "performance" or config `gameinfo.gi` file from the internet. These files change the blocked sections.
4. Close CSDK 12 and every other creator tool before you queue.
5. Remove custom map mods before a normal match. Custom maps and gameplay data mods do not work in Valve's matches.

If the game blocks you, you see this message:

```text
Unable to enter matchmaking while any party member is running Tools-mode, or has unsupported changes to the following sections of their 'gameinfo.gi': Engine2, MaterialSystem2, NetworkSystem, Particles, RenderSystem, SceneSystem, WorldRenderer
```

To fix it, close all creator tools. Then run **Verify integrity of game files** in Steam and redo only the `SearchPaths` edit. [Install mods by hand](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/install-by-hand) shows the edit, or let your mod manager redo it.

## Download safely

### Know what a real mod looks like

- A Deadlock mod is a `.vpk` file. It usually comes inside a `.zip`, `.rar`, or `.7z` archive, often with a readme.
- A mod never needs an `.exe`, `.dll`, `.bat`, `.ps1`, or `.scr` file. If an archive has one, do not open it. Delete the download.
- GameBanana scans every upload with antivirus software and shows the result on the file. This is a first check, not a guarantee.

### Get tools and mods from official pages

| Tool                 | Where to get it                                                                                                                 |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Deadlock Mod Manager | [deadlockmods.app](https://deadlockmods.app) or its [GitHub page](https://github.com/deadlock-mod-manager/deadlock-mod-manager) |
| Source 2 Viewer      | [s2v.app](https://s2v.app/)                                                                                                     |
| CSDK 12              | The [CSDK 12 page](https://deadlockmodding.pages.dev/modding-tools/csdk-12) on Deadlock Modding Notes                           |
| Mods                 | The [Deadlock section](https://gamebanana.com/games/20948) on GameBanana                                                        |

Some creator tools, like CSDK 12, are shared as Google Drive files with no way to check them. Only use the download link on the tool's community page.

### Watch out for fakes

- `deadlockmodmanager.com` is not the official Deadlock Mod Manager site. It is an unofficial lookalike.
- GitHub has fake repos named `deadlock-mod-manager-mods`. They copy the real name but hold no real code.
- GitHub repos that promise a "mod menu", "ESP", or a "Deadlock 2026" toolkit are bait. They are tools for unfair play, or they carry malware. Never download them.
- `steamdb.com` is not SteamDB. The real SteamDB is `steamdb.info`.

## Respect other creators

- Most Deadlock mods use the CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 license. You must give credit, and you may not sell them or share changed versions.
- Ask the author before you edit, reupload, or reuse their work.
- Credit everyone whose work you use. GameBanana holds back mods with missing credits.
- Never sell your mods. Valve's Steam Subscriber Agreement allows only non-commercial use of content made with its tools. GameBanana allows paid early access for 30 days at most, never a full sale.
- Never upload AI-generated art, models, voices, or music to GameBanana's Deadlock section. Its rules ban them.
