Deadlock modding has no official home. Mods live on GameBanana, and most know-how lives in a few Discord servers, written guides, and videos. This page lists the places worth your time and the ones to skip.

## Download mods

GameBanana is where Deadlock creators upload their mods. Nexus Mods has no Deadlock section. The stores inside mod managers show GameBanana's mods too.

1. Open [GameBanana's Deadlock section](https://gamebanana.com/games/20948).
2. Open the [category list](https://gamebanana.com/games/cattree/20948) to browse by type: Skins, HUD, Model Replacement, and more.
3. Check when a mod was last updated. Mods from before the City Never Sleeps update (September 29, 2026) may be broken.
4. Download the mod, or click its 1-Click button to send it to Deadlock Mod Manager.

Sound mods have their own **Sounds** section. Some HUD and Quality of Life mods show information the game hides, like timers. [What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe) explains which mods cross the line.

<ToolCard name="Deadlock Mod Manager" href="https://docs.deadlockmods.app/">
  For problems with the app. The DMM docs link to its own Discord.
</ToolCard>
<ToolCard name="Divine's Discord" href="https://discord.gg/divineskins">
  The Divine team and community hang out here. For Deadlock questions, the
  servers above have more Deadlock creators.
</ToolCard>

To see the Deadlock Modding server's channels, you may need to be in Valve's official Deadlock Discord first. That server is invite-only, and players get the invite from inside the game. If you cannot get into the modding server, use Deadlock GB Community.

## Ask on GameBanana and Reddit

<ToolCard name="GameBanana Questions" href="https://gamebanana.com/games/20948">
  Post a question in the Deadlock section's **Questions** tab. You need a
  GameBanana account, and you must be 13 or older (16 in the EU).
</ToolCard>
<ToolCard
  name="r/DeadlockTheGame"
  href="https://www.reddit.com/r/DeadlockTheGame/"
>
  The main Deadlock subreddit, for showing off mods and asking questions. Its
  rules ask you to credit creators and ban AI-made content.
</ToolCard>

There is no general modding subreddit. The official Deadlock forums and deadlock.wiki have no modding sections.

## Sites to skip

- **Sites that copy GameBanana's listings.** Download from GameBanana itself, where the creator's page, credits, and updates live. Some creators have asked these copy sites to take their mods down.
- **`steamdb.com`.** It is not SteamDB. The real SteamDB is `steamdb.info`.
- **`deadlockmodmanager.com`.** It is an unofficial lookalike of Deadlock Mod Manager's site.

[Download safely](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe#download-safely) lists more fakes to watch for.
