Deadlock modding has no official home. Mods live on GameBanana, and most know-how lives in a few Discord servers, written guides, and videos. This page lists the places worth your time and the ones to skip.

## Download mods

GameBanana is where Deadlock creators upload their mods. Nexus Mods has no Deadlock section. The stores inside mod managers show GameBanana's mods too.

1. Open [GameBanana's Deadlock section](https://gamebanana.com/games/20948).
2. Open the [category list](https://gamebanana.com/games/cattree/20948) to browse by type: Skins, HUD, Model Replacement, and more.
3. Check when a mod was last updated. Mods from before the City Never Sleeps update (September 29, 2026) may be broken.
4. Download the mod, or click its 1-Click button to send it to Deadlock Mod Manager.

Sound mods have their own **Sounds** section. Some HUD and Quality of Life mods show information the game hides, like timers. [What's allowed and how to stay safe](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe) explains which mods cross the line.

## Read written guides

Start with this wiki. When you need more depth, these are the guides creators link most:

- **[Deadlock Modding Notes](https://deadlockmodding.pages.dev/):** a community site with guides for CSDK 12, packing, UI images, sounds, and animations. Its install page adds `gameinfo.gi` lines that do not belong in the game's file. Follow [Install mods by hand](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/install-by-hand) for that part instead.
- **[Fuji's Asset Replacement Guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/10Be28UBdeN13R_-NO5PqYI7SiCyklldGK_JkZQUqAAg/edit):** the most detailed written guide to model swaps, with a troubleshooting section.
- **[Neutral's General Guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1p3_Q3HXRLmQOWnBvkUa5BljGmHVvW33UjBqEz-WRk24/edit):** models, textures, and sounds in one document.
- **[GameBanana tutorials](https://gamebanana.com/tuts/games/20948):** a short list of Deadlock tutorials, like Axelliax's "for Dummies" guides to image and model replacement.

Most of these were written before City Never Sleeps. Some steps may have changed since.

## Watch video guides

[Deadlock Modding Tutorials](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLhHSf4OKyoHA1qstq1Uqd4R4QMgo-j6Lp) is a 20-video playlist by Pugzilla. It covers model swaps, sounds, icons, particles, and more, and it collects videos by other creators too.

Every video about making mods that we found was recorded before City Never Sleeps. Menus in the videos may look different from yours.

## Ask on Discord

- **[Deadlock Modding](https://discord.gg/P2PCCtYt2B):** the main creator server, with 21,000+ members. Ask for help here, read the guides channel, and find tools that are shared only there. To see its channels, you may need to be in Valve's official Deadlock Discord first. That server is invite-only, and players get the invite from inside the game.
- **[Deadlock GB Community](https://discord.gg/CaNPxMQeFN):** a public server run by GameBanana's Deadlock section managers. Ask about GameBanana's rules and get feedback on your uploads. It is also the place to go if you cannot get into the modding server.
- **Deadlock Mod Manager:** for problems with the app, the [DMM docs](https://docs.deadlockmods.app/) link to its own Discord.
- **[Divine's Discord](https://discord.gg/divineskins):** the Divine team and community hang out here. For Deadlock questions, the servers above have more Deadlock creators.

## Ask on GameBanana and Reddit

- **GameBanana Questions:** post a question in the Deadlock section's **Questions** tab. You need a GameBanana account, and you must be 13 or older (16 in the EU).
- **[r/DeadlockTheGame](https://www.reddit.com/r/DeadlockTheGame/):** the main Deadlock subreddit, for showing off mods and asking questions. There is no general modding subreddit. Its rules ask you to credit creators and ban AI-made content.

The official Deadlock forums and deadlock.wiki have no modding sections.

## Sites to skip

- **Sites that copy GameBanana's listings.** Download from GameBanana itself, where the creator's page, credits, and updates live. Some creators have asked these copy sites to take their mods down.
- **`steamdb.com`.** It is not SteamDB. The real SteamDB is `steamdb.info`.
- **`deadlockmodmanager.com`.** It is an unofficial lookalike of Deadlock Mod Manager's site.

[Download safely](/docs/deadlock/getting-started/stay-safe#download-safely) lists more fakes to watch for.
